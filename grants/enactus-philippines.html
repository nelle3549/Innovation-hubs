Enactus Philippines (national competition → Enactus World Cup)

Enactus Philippines (national member organisation), feeding the global Enactus World Cup

Enactus runs student-led social enterprise programmes through university chapters worldwide. In the Philippines, Enactus Philippines is the national member organisation and handles the country's representation.

Overview

🇵🇭 How Philippine access actually works — one representative per country. Enactus Philippines runs the national round. The team that wins nationally becomes the Philippine representative and goes on to compete at the Enactus World Cup. You do not enter the World Cup directly; you win your way there through Enactus Philippines.

🔗 Paired page: the incubation arm is catalogued in Innovation Centers as Enactus — Incubation Arm (Social Startup Incubator / EnAccelerator). Incubation builds the venture; acceleration funds and stages it. Students normally pass through incubation first.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • University/tertiary students. There is no global Enactus high-school track — BED students cannot enter.
  • Students compete through their university's Enactus chapter, under Enactus Philippines.
  • Only the national champion advances to the World Cup.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive.

  • National round (Enactus Philippines) — the pathway that matters for Philippine teams. The national champion earns the country slot at the World Cup.
  • Project Accelerators — Enactus teams apply for funding and/or mentorship to develop projects addressing a specific need or community. Amounts vary by accelerator and sponsor. Example: the Alterna Savings SDG Project Accelerator.
  • Enactus World Cup — recognition and trophy. ⚠️ No direct cash award to teams. The value is exposure, network, and credibility.

💰 The money sits in the Project Accelerators, not the World Cup. Set expectations accordingly: the World Cup is a stage, not a cheque.

Key dates & deadline

Annual, structured around the national round, with the World Cup following. Project Accelerator calls open on their own sponsor schedules.

📅 Forecasted application window: Annual national round, then World Cup.

How to apply

Through a university's Enactus chapter, registered under Enactus Philippines. There is no direct individual entry, and no direct entry to the World Cup.
