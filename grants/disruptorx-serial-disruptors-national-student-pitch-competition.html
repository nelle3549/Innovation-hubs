DisruptorX — Serial Disruptors National Student Pitch Competition

Serial Disruptors

DisruptorX is the annual national pitching competition run by Serial Disruptors — the company behind the Startupreneurship courseware and the Startupreneur 4-in-1 e-learning app. It surfaces the strongest student-led ventures across Serial Disruptors' partner schools and gives high school and college teams a national stage to pitch their startups to real judges, investors, educators, and industry leaders.

Overview

The competition is positioned as the culminating showcase of the Startupreneurship ecosystem: students learn innovation and technopreneurship inside their school curriculum, develop ideas through the Innovation Hub, and the best ideas compete once a year at DisruptorX.

How it works

DisruptorX runs as a multi-month cycle: applications open to student teams, entries are narrowed through two rounds of shortlisting into a set of finalists (typically 10 per category), and finalists advance to a live Demo Day where they pitch to a panel of judges, industry leaders, and prospective partners. Winners receive cash prizes and continued exposure to mentors and investors.

DisruptorX 2026 (most recent cycle)

The 2026 Demo Day was held May 25, 2026 at Limketkai Luxe Hotel, Cagayan de Oro City, with a total prize pool of ₱600,000 and 20 finalist teams (10 Basic Education, 10 College).

Application funnel (internal, per April 2026 board report):

  • 305 total applications received — 241 College, 64 Basic Education. Strongest top-of-funnel to date.
  • Round 1 shortlist: 60 teams (top 30 per category).
  • Round 2 finalists: 22 (12 College incl. 2 wildcards, 10 BED).
  • 28 schools represented in College; 15 schools in Basic Education.
  • Top College entries: University of the Cordilleras (49), Bulacan State University (21), Ateneo de Davao University (20), Davao Central College (20), Central Philippine University (14), West Visayas State University (14).

2026 Grand Winners:

  • College — Pixcell (Cebu Institute of Technology): Thomas Danjo Manulat, Simon Lyster Escano, Malt John Vianney Solon; coach Jun Albert Pardillo. An AI-powered microscopy plug-in camera that works offline with any standard microscope and laptop, capturing slides, running AI anomaly detection in seconds, and generating automated reports for medical diagnostics.
  • Basic Education — HuskUp (De La Salle University Senior High School): Jaime Luis de Guia, Aaron Luis Plata, Jian Loriz Santorcas; coach Mary Geraldine Yumang. A portable water filter using rice husk biochar, gravel, sand and cotton mesh layers with a solar UVC sterilization lid, for off-grid households, schools, and disaster relief.

DisruptorX 2025 (prior cycle)

  • 242 applications from 20+ schools across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.
  • Top 10 finalists announced in both Basic Education and College categories.
  • Demo Day held June 30, 2025 at Chali Beach Resort, Cagayan de Oro City.
  • Explicitly aligned to SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure), and SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals).
  • Example finalist teams: University of the Cordilleras fielded B.O.S BLOCKS (Basic Education), Turomi and The AERO-Force (College).
