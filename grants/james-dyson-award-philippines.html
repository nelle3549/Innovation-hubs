James Dyson Award — Philippines

James Dyson Foundation

The James Dyson Award is an international design engineering competition run by the James Dyson Foundation, open across 28 countries and regions — including the Philippines, which runs its own national judging round. The brief is deliberately broad: design something that solves a problem. Filipino entrants compete first at national level, and national winners progress to the international stage judged by Sir James Dyson himself.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Current university students of engineering, product design, or industrial design, or those who graduated in these subjects within the last four years.
  • Team entries welcome: if you studied a different subject, you may still enter as part of a team, provided the team leader studied a qualifying subject.
  • All team members must be — or have been within the last four years — enrolled for at least one semester in an undergraduate or graduate programme.
  • Entrants must be from one of the 28 participating countries/regions. The Philippines is a participating country with its own national round.

⚠️ Basic Education teams are ineligible. University enrolment (or graduation within 4 years) is required. Like Hult Prize, this is college-and-above only.

Funding / award

All prizes are non-dilutive — no equity is taken.

📅 Forecasted application window: The cycle typically opens around March and closes mid-July. Expect the 2027 call to open ≈ March 2027 and close ≈ mid-July 2027.

How to apply

Submit online via the Philippine entry portal at jamesdysonaward.org/en-PH. Entries require an explanation of the problem, the solution, the design process, and supporting images/sketches. Judges reward clear evidence of iterative prototyping and a demonstrated problem-solving process — not just a polished final render.
