Blue Ocean Student Entrepreneur Competition

Blue Ocean is an exclusively high-school entrepreneurship competition, entirely virtual, requiring no travel. Its 2026 edition drew 23,000+ students from 173 countries, making it one of the largest high-school entrepreneurship competitions in the world.

Overview

🎓 BED ELIGIBLE — and high-school ONLY. Ages 14–18, enrolled in high school. There is no university track, so BED students are not competing against college teams.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Ages 14–18, currently enrolled in high school.
  • Open worldwide — 173 countries represented in 2026.
  • Fully virtual — no travel required, which removes the cost barrier that blocks most Philippine teams from international competitions.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive cash prizes. No equity.

Key dates & deadline

Annual.

How to apply

Submit a pitch via blueoceancompetition.org. Entry is virtual end to end.
