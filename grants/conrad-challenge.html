Conrad Challenge

Conrad Foundation (Space Center Houston)

The Conrad Challenge is an annual, multi-round innovation and entrepreneurship competition for students aged 13–18, run by the Conrad Foundation in partnership with Space Center Houston. Teams build science- and technology-driven commercial solutions in categories such as aerospace, energy, health, cyber, and sustainability.

Overview

🎓 HIGH SCHOOL ELIGIBLE — ages 13–18. "Teams may compete from anywhere in the world." No US or institutional affiliation required.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Students aged 13–18, in high school or homeschool.
  • Teams of 2–5 students, plus an adult coach.
  • Open worldwide — teams may compete from anywhere.

No university enrolment required. Directly open to Basic Education teams.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive. Winning teams are named Pete Conrad Scholars and receive a package that has historically included seed funding, patent/IP support, scholarships, and in-kind services.

Key dates & deadline

Annual, multi-round, running across the academic year from autumn registration to a spring Innovation Summit.

📅 Forecasted application window: Registration typically opens in the autumn, with staged round deadlines through the year.

How to apply

Register a team and coach at conradchallenge.org. The competition runs in staged rounds across the academic year, culminating in the Innovation Summit for finalists.
