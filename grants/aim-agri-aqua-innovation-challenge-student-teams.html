AIM Agri-Aqua Innovation Challenge — Student Teams

Asian Institute of Management (AIM), with DOST agri-aqua Technology Business Incubators (ATBIs)

The Student Teams track of the AIM Agri-Aqua Innovation Challenge is open to Filipino college and graduate students from any school — it is not restricted to AIM students. This makes it one of the most directly relevant national opportunities for Startupreneur college teams working on agri-aqua problems.

Organization
Asian Institute of Management (AIM), with DOST agri-aqua Technology Business Incubators (ATBIs)
Coverage
National
Funding / award
Non-dilutive. Demo Day investment grants (less tax): at least PHP 100,000 per student-team finalist; Grand winner PHP 400,000. Host ATBI receives PHP 50,000 per incubatee reaching the final round. Additional seed funding provided during the program.
Funding stage
Idea / Pre-startup, Non-dilutive grant, Pre-seed, Prize / non-dilutive
Sector focus
Agritech, Climate / Sustainability, Deep tech

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Filipino citizen, currently a college or graduate student at any school.
  • Technology at TRL 4 or above.
  • Excluded: prior finalists and prior DOST Startup Grant Fund recipients.

🔑 A DOST ATBI endorsement letter is required to pitch and qualify. This is the single gating requirement.

⚠️ Basic Education teams are ineligible — college or graduate enrolment required.

Secure an ATBI endorsement letter first, then apply via aim.edu/agri-aqua-innovation-challenge.
