NFTE World Series of Innovation

Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship (NFTE)

The NFTE World Series of Innovation runs multiple sponsor-backed innovation challenges, each tied to a UN Sustainable Development Goal. Students submit solutions to specific briefs rather than building a full startup, which lowers the entry bar considerably.

Overview

🎓 BED ELIGIBLE. The Impact League covers ages 13–24, and an Imagination League covers ages 5–12. Students may compete solo or in teams, and may enter through a school or independently. Active in 50+ countries.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Impact League: ages 13–24 — spans junior high through college.
  • Imagination League: ages 5–12 — reaches elementary.
  • Solo or team entries; school affiliation optional.
  • Open globally.

This is the only program found that explicitly serves ages 5–12, alongside Technovation's 8–18.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive cash prizes: USD 300 – USD 1,500 per challenge. Modest amounts, but there are many challenges, each separately winnable.

Key dates & deadline

Annual, with rolling challenge-specific deadlines across the cycle.

📅 Forecasted application window: Challenges open through the academic year.

How to apply

Pick an open challenge at innovation.nfte.com and submit against that brief. Each challenge has its own deadline.
