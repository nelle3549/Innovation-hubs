HKUST One Million — International Student Track

HKUST Entrepreneurship Center (HKUST-Sino One Million Dollar Entrepreneurship Competition)

The International Student Track of the HKUST-Sino One Million Dollar Entrepreneurship Competition is explicitly open to students from non-local (non-Hong Kong) universities worldwide — including the Philippines. It is the sibling of the HKUST Track, which is restricted to HKUST affiliates.

Overview

🚨 LIVE DEADLINE — Round 1 closes 14 July 2026.

⚠️ Do not confuse the two tracks. The HKUST Track requires HKUST students/alumni/staff and that HKUST members hold ≥20% ownership — Filipinos cannot enter it. Only this International Student Track is open. (The HKUST Track is filed in Archive Index as institution-restricted.)

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Team must include at least one current full-time undergraduate or postgraduate student at a non-local (international) university, graduating no earlier than Spring 2026.
  • Post-docs are excluded.
  • The winning team must register a company (in Hong Kong or its home country) in order to receive the cash award.

⚠️ Basic Education teams are ineligible — university enrolment is required.

Funding / award

All prizes are non-dilutive cash — official terms state "the equity of the winning companies should not be changed."

Key dates & deadline

  • Round 1 online application / pitch deck: due 14 July 2026.
  • Judging rounds: July – October 2026.
  • Grand Final: Hong Kong, 12–13 October 2026.

📅 Forecasted application window: Annual, with the Round 1 deadline in mid-July and the final in October. Expect the 2027 call on the same rhythm.
