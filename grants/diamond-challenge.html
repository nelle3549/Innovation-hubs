Diamond Challenge (High School Entrepreneurship)

Horn Entrepreneurship, University of Delaware

The Diamond Challenge, run by the University of Delaware's Horn Entrepreneurship, is one of the largest high-school-level entrepreneurship competitions in the world. It runs two tracks: Business Innovation and Social Innovation.

Organization
Horn Entrepreneurship, University of Delaware

Sector-agnostic, Social impact / SDG

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Teams of 2–4 students, aged 14–18, enrolled in high school (or equivalent) anywhere in the world.
  • Each team requires an adult advisor aged 21+ (a teacher qualifies).
  • No prior business required — this is an idea-stage competition.

No university enrolment required. No citizenship restriction.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive cash prizes, awarded across both tracks. No equity is taken.

Key dates & deadline

  • 2026 cycle: submission deadline was January 15, 2026.

📅 Forecasted application window: Annual — registration opens in the autumn, closing mid-January. Expect the next deadline ~January 2027.

How to apply

Register a team and an adult advisor at diamondchallenge.org/competition. Teams submit a written concept, then advance to pitch rounds.
