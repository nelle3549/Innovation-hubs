Fowler Global Social Innovation Challenge (GSIC)

University of San Diego — Center for Peace and Commerce

The Fowler Global Social Innovation Challenge (GSIC), run by the University of San Diego's Center for Peace and Commerce, is a global student social-venture competition. Its 2026 edition drew student entrepreneurs from 15 countries.

Funding / award
USD 100,000 total pool of non-dilutive seed funding plus in-kind professional services, shared among the top six finalists; 16+ teams receive non-dilutive funding overall (2026 figures). Older pages still cite 'USD 50,000+'.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • At least one team member must be a current undergraduate, graduate, or doctoral student.
  • No citizenship restriction.

The sign-up page indicates a student may apply regardless of whether their college is a GSIC partner institution. The earlier FAQ wording suggesting a "participating institution" requirement is treated as outdated or misleading.

Philippine students at any university can enter. No school-level enrolment in the programme appears to be needed first.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive. A USD 100,000 total pool of non-dilutive seed funding plus in-kind professional services, shared among the top six finalists; 16+ teams receive non-dilutive funding overall.

Key dates & deadline

  • 2026 cycle: CLOSED. Pitch decks were due April 24, 2026; Global Finals held May 1–3, 2026.
  • 2027 cycle: not yet posted.

📅 Forecasted application window: Annual — applications and preliminary rounds run fall through spring, with the deadline in late April and Global Finals in early May. Expect the 2027 call on the same rhythm.

How to apply

Apply via sandiego.edu/cpc/gsic. Preliminary rounds run through the academic year, culminating in the Global Finals.
