Technovation Girls

Technovation

Technovation Girls is a global technology-entrepreneurship program in which girls and gender-expansive youth build a mobile app or AI solution addressing a community problem, supported by a mentor.

Overview

🎓 HIGH SCHOOL AND YOUNGER — ages 8–18. The widest age range of any program. It reaches elementary and junior high as well as senior high.

🇵🇭 The Philippines is a named focus/scaling country for Technovation.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Students aged 8–18 (assessed as of August 1 of the cycle year).
  • Participants must identify as female, nonbinary, or gender-nonconforming.
  • Teams work with a mentor; no prior coding experience required.

⚠️ Gender-restricted. This is not open to all students — it is specifically for girls and gender-expansive youth. Plan mixed-gender classes accordingly.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive. Scholarships, prizes, and recognition awarded by age division. No equity.

Key dates & deadline

Annual. Age is assessed as of August 1 of the cycle year. The season typically runs January–April with spring submissions.

📅 Forecasted application window: Registration opens in the autumn/winter; submissions close in spring.

How to apply

Register teams and mentors at technovationchallenge.org/competition. Teams submit an app/AI prototype, a pitch video, and a business plan.
