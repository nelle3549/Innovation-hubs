Zayed Sustainability Prize

Government of the United Arab Emirates

The Zayed Sustainability Prize is the UAE's global award for sustainability and humanitarian solutions. It spans six categories — including a dedicated Global High Schools category, which is what makes it relevant here.

Organization
Government of the United Arab Emirates

Funding / award
Non-dilutive. Global High Schools category: USD 150,000 per winning school (six world regions). SME and Non-Profit categories: USD 1,000,000 each per category.

Overview

🎓 HIGH SCHOOL ELIGIBLE via the Global High Schools category, and the money is serious: USD 150,000 per winning school, awarded across six world regions. The Philippines competes in the Asia region.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Global High Schools: secondary schools worldwide may submit a student-led sustainability project. Filipino schools compete in the Asia region.
  • SME / NPO categories: registered organisations with a track record of impact.

No university enrolment required for the high-school category. This is a school-level entry — the institution applies, not an individual.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive. No equity taken.

The high-school award funds the school to implement or expand its own sustainability project.

Key dates & deadline

Annual. Submissions typically open in the first half of the year and close mid-year; winners are announced at Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week in January.

📅 Forecasted application window: Expect submissions to open ~H1 and close ~mid-year, with January winners.

How to apply

Submit via zayedsustainabilityprize.com/en/how-to-apply. The high-school entry is made by the school, describing a student-led project and how the USD 150,000 would be deployed.
