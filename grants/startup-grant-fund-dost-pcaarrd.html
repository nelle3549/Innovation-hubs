Overview

The Startup Grant Fund (SGF) of DOST-PCAARRD provides financial and technical support to startups and spinoff companies commercializing technologies in the Agriculture, Aquatic and Natural Resources (AANR) sector, pushing them toward market readiness. It is managed by PCAARRD's Technology Transfer and Promotion Division (TTPD). Since 2021 the program has supported 27 startups, awarding a total of about PHP 64M in grants. It prioritizes ventures with strong market-adoption potential and measurable contributions to national development.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Startup and spinoff companies operating in the agriculture, aquatic, and natural-resources sector.
  • Strong potential for market adoption and a clear commercialization pathway.
  • Technology should align with PCAARRD's AANR priorities.
  • Student-friendly relative to peers — agri/aqua student ventures with a registered or near-registered company and a real product can be competitive.

Funding / award

  • Form: Non-refundable grant plus technical/mentoring support.
  • Amount: Up to PHP 5M per startup (per the 2025/2026 SGF guidance).
  • Use: Commercialization activities for AANR-sector technologies (scale-up, market validation, product readiness).

Key dates & deadline

  • 2026 cycle: submissions Feb 16 – Apr 15, 2026 (confirmed on the official PCAARRD call announcement). As of June 30, 2026, this window has closed.
  • Annual program — a similar Q1 window is expected for the next (2027) cycle.

📅 Forecasted application window: Next call likely opens ≈ Feb–Apr 2027, mirroring the 2026 cycle (Feb 16 – Apr 15).

How to apply

  • Submit a proposal per the official call guidelines published on the PCAARRD QID article / Tech Transfer channels.
  • Documents: Proposal, commercialization plan, company/registration details, and supporting technical documentation.
  • Contact: PCAARRD Technology Transfer and Promotion Division (TTPD).
  • Portals: PCAARRD 2026 SGF call.
