NASA Space Apps Challenge

NASA (with international space agency partners)

The NASA Space Apps Challenge is the world's largest annual global hackathon, run by NASA with international space-agency partners across 167 countries and territories. Teams solve real challenges using open NASA data over a single weekend. Philippine local events are hosted each year.

Overview

🎓 NO MINIMUM AGE. This is the only program with no age floor at all. Under-18s must be registered by a parent or legal guardian and accompanied by one at all times. That makes it accessible from elementary through professional.

Qualifications & eligibility

  • Open to anyone, regardless of age or experience.
  • Under-18s must be registered by a parent/legal guardian and accompanied by one at all times.
  • Participation is via a local host event (the Philippines hosts these) or virtually.
  • US sanctions exclude a handful of countries; the Philippines is not among them.

Funding / award

⚠️ Non-monetary. There is no cash prize. Winners receive Global Nominee status, international recognition, and — for global winners — a visit to a NASA facility.

The value is prestige, experience, and a credential, not money. Set expectations accordingly.

Key dates & deadline

Annual, historically a weekend in early October.

📅 Forecasted application window: Registration typically opens late summer for an October event.

How to apply

Register for a local or virtual event at spaceappschallenge.org. Teams form around published challenges and build over the hackathon weekend.
