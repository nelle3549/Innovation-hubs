Youth Co:Lab Springboard

UNDP and Citi Foundation

Youth Co:Lab Springboard is UNDP and Citi Foundation's youth social-entrepreneurship program across Asia-Pacific, of which the Philippines is part.

Qualifications & eligibility

AGE FLOOR RESOLVED (July 9, 2026): core team members must be aged 15–30.

🎓 This makes Youth Co:Lab BED-eligible from age 15 — i.e. senior high school. It is the only regional (Asia-Pacific) programme open to Basic Education students; every other BED option is either global or domestic.

  • Core team members: aged 15–30.
  • Asia-Pacific focus; the Philippines is in scope.
  • Spans BED (15+), college, and young professionals in a single programme.

Funding / award

Non-dilutive seed grants, amount varying by country edition, plus training and ecosystem support.

Key dates & deadline

Recurring, per country.

How to apply

Entry is via the country-level Youth Co:Lab edition. See youthcolab.org/springboard and contact UNDP Philippines to confirm the local age floor and cycle.
