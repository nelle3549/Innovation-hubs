Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

DLSU-D CEAT Center of Technology (CCTI Fab Lab)

De La Salle University-Dasmariñas · Region IV-A

The DLSU-D SSF Innovation Laboratory — branded the CEAT Center for Technological Innovation (CCTI) — is a fabrication laboratory hosted by De La Salle University-Dasmariñas within its College of Engineering, Architecture, and Technology (CEAT) in Dasmariñas, Cavite (Region IV-A). Established under the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) Shared Service Facility (SSF) Program through a Memorandum of Agreement signed with DTI-Region 4A in April 2021, the P24-million InnoLab held its soft launch on December 21, 2021, and stands as one of the first major fabrication laboratories in Cavite.

About the Center

The facility is equipped with an array of state-of-the-art computer-aided machines with AI capabilities that can work across various materials and length scales. It serves the college's research units, MSMEs, industry and community partners, technopreneurs, students, and enthusiasts of digital design and fabrication, enabling the transformation of digital designs into solid prototypes.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing of prototypes, components, and product models.
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision cutting and engraving across a range of materials.
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Computer-aided machining for fabrication and prototyping.
  • Vinyl Cutting — Production of stickers, labels, and signage.
  • Electronics & PCB Prototyping — Support for developing and testing electronic designs.
  • Design & Prototyping Support — Assistance converting digital designs into physical prototypes for MSMEs, technopreneurs, and students.
  • Trainings & Facility Access — Capability-building and shared access to advanced fabrication equipment for Cavite enterprises and the university community.
