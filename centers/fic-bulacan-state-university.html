Food Innovation Center

FIC — Bulacan State University

Bulacan State University · Region III

The Bulacan State University Food Innovation Center (BulSU FIC) is a DOST-HITS (Harmonized Industrial Technology Services) recipient at Bulacan State University (BulSU) in Malolos, Bulacan, providing food product development and innovation services to MSMEs in Region III. Operating under BulSU's Office of VP for Research, Extension, and Innovation (OVPREI), the FIC serves as the university's flagship food technology hub — supporting food entrepreneurs and producers across Bulacan and the Central Luzon region.

About the Center

The center is equipped with the standard DOST FIC machinery suite — spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer — enabling MSMEs to scale up production processes, develop value-added food products, and meet food safety and regulatory requirements.

Programs & Services

  • Food product development and reformulation for MSMEs
  • Food processing using spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer
  • Food safety and regulatory assistance (FDA registration, labeling compliance)
  • Packaging design and nutrition labeling support
  • Product costing and market analysis
  • Food product testing and quality evaluation
  • Equipment rental and access for production runs
  • Training programs in food technology, food safety, and food entrepreneurship
