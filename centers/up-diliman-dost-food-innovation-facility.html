Food Innovation Center

UP Diliman–DOST Food Innovation Facility (FIF)

University of the Philippines Diliman · NCR

The Food Innovation Facility (FIF) at the University of the Philippines Diliman is the Metro Manila–based center in the DOST national Food Innovation Center (FIC) network. Hosted by UP Diliman's College of Home Economics through the Department of Food Science and Nutrition (CHE-DFSN), the FIF is co-located with the Pilot Food Plant on the UP Diliman campus — giving it direct access to both research-grade processing infrastructure and the scientific expertise of the country's premier state university.

About the Center

Originally designated as the NCR Regional Food Innovation Center, the center has since been renamed the Food Innovation Facility (FIF) to reflect its evolved mandate as a research-driven hub embedded within an academic setting. It serves MSMEs across Metro Manila and the National Capital Region, providing access to DOST-standard food processing technologies and the R&D depth of UP Diliman's food science faculty and researchers.

The CHE-DFSN's research focus spans food product development, nutritional science, food safety, and value-added processing — making the FIF a uniquely capable center for NCR-based food entrepreneurs seeking to develop, validate, and scale new products backed by academic rigor.

Programs & Services

  • Product and process R&D using DOST-standard food processing equipment including freeze dryer, spray dryer, vacuum fryer, and water retort
  • Pilot food plant access for scale-up trials, process validation, and batch production runs
  • Technical consultancy from CHE-DFSN faculty and researchers for food product development and formulation
  • Food safety and quality assurance guidance aligned with FDA Philippines standards
  • Nutritional analysis and food characterization services for MSME product development
  • Packaging design and regulatory labeling consultancy to help products reach market readiness
  • Technology training programs for food entrepreneurs and processing industry personnel in NCR
  • Academic-industry R&D collaboration for MSMEs seeking research partnerships with UP Diliman
