North Eastern Mindanao State University (NEMSU) · Region XIII

The NEMSU Food Innovation Center at North Eastern Mindanao State University (formerly Surigao del Sur State University) is one of the five Food Innovation Centers operated by DOST Caraga (with CSU, SNSU, ASSCAT and FSUU). Its reported FIC activity is based at the NEMSU Lianga campus in Surigao del Sur; the university's main office is in Tandag.

About the Center

The Caraga FIC at North Eastern Mindanao State University (NEMSU FIC) is a DOST-designated Food Innovation Center hosted by North Eastern Mindanao State University in Tandag City, Surigao del Sur. As a member of the Caraga Food Innovation Consortium (CFIC) coordinated with DOST-Caraga, the NEMSU FIC serves the food processing and agri-product development needs of MSMEs across Surigao del Sur and the broader Caraga region (Region XIII).

North Eastern Mindanao State University (formerly SDSSU), the leading state university of Surigao del Sur, provides the institutional anchor and food science expertise for the FIC. The center equips MSMEs and food entrepreneurs in this resource-rich region — where coastal fisheries, root crops, tropical fruits, and agri-based industries represent strong opportunities for value-added processing. As the easternmost FIC in Mindanao, NEMSU FIC plays a critical role in extending DOST food innovation support to communities in Surigao del Sur.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Formulation assistance, recipe optimization, and product development support for new and improved food products from Surigao del Sur producers
  • Food Processing Services — Contract processing using standard FIC equipment — spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer — for MSME clients developing shelf-stable food products
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Guidance — Assistance with FDA product registration, labeling compliance, and certification pathways
  • Packaging Design & Nutrition Labeling — Support for packaging development, branding, and nutritional information for commercial food products
  • Product Costing & Market Analysis — Assistance with production cost computation, pricing strategy, and market feasibility for food enterprises
  • Product Testing & Quality Assurance — Laboratory testing and quality control support for food safety, shelf-life, and standards compliance
  • Equipment Rental — Access to DOST FIC processing equipment for MSMEs needing processing capacity
  • MSME Training & Capacity-Building — Seminars, workshops, and technical training for Caraga food producers in food technology, food safety, and enterprise development
