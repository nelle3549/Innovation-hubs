UPLB SIBOL Labs (UPLB TTBDO)

UP Los Baños Technology Transfer and Business Development Office (TTBDO) · Region IV-A

UPLB SIBOL (Startup Innovation and Business Opportunity Linkage) Innovation Hub is the University of the Philippines Los Baños's premier business incubation program, operating as the commercialization and startup support arm of the UPLB Technology Transfer and Business Development Office (UP TTBDO). Located in Los Baños, Laguna — at the heart of one of the Philippines' most concentrated agri-science research ecosystems — SIBOL provides a structured incubation pathway for startup enterprises emerging from UPLB's agricultural biotechnology, food systems, dairy science, and information and communications technology research programs.

About the Center

SIBOL operates in close coordination with UPLB's S&T Park and the UP TTBDO, enabling startups and spin-offs access to UPLB's research infrastructure, biological collections, pilot processing facilities, and expert faculty mentors. Incubatees gain access to IP support (via UP TTBDO), market development coaching, investor linkage, and government program facilitation — including DOST SETUP grants, DTI Small Business Corporation financing, and Department of Agriculture technology adoption programs. SIBOL's portfolio spans agri-biotech ventures, food and nutraceutical startups, dairy enterprise development, and ICT solutions for agriculture.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation — Structured startup incubation with workspace, mentoring, business development coaching, and milestone-based support for technology-based enterprises emerging from UPLB research
  • Agri-Biotech Startup Support — Specialized incubation for ventures in plant breeding, bio-inputs, biofertilizers, biopesticides, and agri-biotechnology spin-offs from UPLB's research institutes
  • ICT for Agriculture — Incubation for tech startups developing digital tools, precision agriculture platforms, and agri-data solutions for the Philippine agriculture sector
  • IP Management & Technology Licensing (via UP TTBDO) — Intellectual property support, patent prosecution, and technology licensing facilitation for SIBOL incubatees through the UP TTBDO's IP portfolio management services
  • Investment & Partnership Linkage — Investor matchmaking, DOST SETUP facilitation, and industry partnership brokering to accelerate commercialization of SIBOL ventures
