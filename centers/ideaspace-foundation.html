IdeaSpace Foundation · NCR

IdeaSpace Foundation is a non-profit technology business incubator and startup accelerator based in Makati City, Metro Manila. Established in 2012 and backed by the MVP Group of Companies — including First Pacific, Metro Pacific Investments Corporation, PLDT-Smart, and Meralco — it supports early-stage Filipino tech entrepreneurs solving problems in emerging markets.

About the Center

Through its flagship Accelerator Program, IdeaSpace has supported over 150 startups, providing funding, mentorship, and ecosystem access. Its investment activities operate under Ideaspace Ventures, and it remains one of the most established players in the Philippine startup ecosystem, with its 13th cohort concluding in late 2025.

Programs & Services

  • Accelerator Program — A four-month hybrid program for scaling startups, offering mentorship, funding opportunities, and strategic exposure, culminating in a Demo Day.
  • Seed Funding — Early-stage capital investment provided to selected portfolio startups to help them build and scale.
  • Mentorship Network — Access to industry experts, seasoned entrepreneurs, and potential investors for one-on-one guidance.
  • Workspace & Facilities — Office space and physical resources for participating startup teams.
  • Investor & Network Access — Connections to a wide network of corporate partners, investors, and the broader innovation ecosystem.
  • Workshops & Ecosystem Events — Specialized workshops, networking events, and awareness roadshows on innovation and technopreneurship.
