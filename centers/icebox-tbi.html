Technology Business Incubator

ICEBOX TBI

MSU-General Santos · Region XII

ICEBOX TBI (Innovation Center for Enterprises and Business Opportunities) is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Mindanao State University - General Santos (MSU-GenSan) in General Santos City, Region XII (SOCCSKSARGEN). Described as one of its kind in Region XII, ICEBOX is a DOST-assisted TBI focused on Smart Systems Innovations, providing startups with the support mechanisms and resources they need to transform ideas into prototypes and, ultimately, viable techno-entrepreneurial ventures.

About the Center

Funded by the DOST-Philippine Council for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD) under the HEIRIT program, ICEBOX is operated by MSU-GenSan to foster innovation and technopreneurship in the region. Its work spans problem identification, consultation, ideation, incubation, prototyping, and packaging for commercialization, and it has supported early startup ventures within the SOCCSKSARGEN innovation community.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — Structured support helping startups move from idea to prototype to market-ready venture.
  • Pre-Incubation & Ideation — Problem identification, consultation, and early concept development for aspiring technopreneurs.
  • Prototyping Support — Assistance in building prototypes for smart systems and technology-based products.
  • Mentoring & Advisory — Coaching from mentors, technical experts, and business advisors.
  • Technopreneurship Training — Capacity-building on innovation, business modeling, and enterprise development.
  • Packaging & Commercialization Support — Guidance on preparing products for market and commercialization.
  • DOST-PCIEERD Grant & Funding Linkage — Assistance in accessing DOST seed funding and startup grants.
  • Market & Network Linkage — Connections to markets, investors, and the regional innovation ecosystem.
