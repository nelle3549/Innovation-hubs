Food Innovation Center

PSU-DOST 1 FIC — Pangasinan State University

Pangasinan State University · Region I

The PSU-DOST 1 Food Innovation Center is hosted by Pangasinan State University at its Bayambang Campus in Bayambang, Pangasinan and serves Region I (Ilocos Region). One of PSU's ten flagship Research, Extension, and Innovation Centers, the PSU-DOST 1 FIC operates as the region's dedicated food processing and innovation hub — enabling Pangasinan's food producers, entrepreneurs, students, and researchers to access DOST-funded technology, equipment, and expertise for value-adding agri-fishery products.

About the Center

The center functions as a one-stop food R&D facility, bridging Pangasinan's significant agricultural production base — covering Pangasinan Bay bangus (milkfish), onion, garlic, mango, and a diversity of upland crops — with the food processing technology and business development support needed to take products from raw material to market-ready item. Pangasinan's position as one of the Philippines' top bangus and onion producers makes the FIC's food processing capabilities particularly valuable to the region's MSME ecosystem.

The PSU-DOST 1 FIC has developed a strong portfolio of food innovations including vacuum fried products (squash, onion, mushroom, okra), binagoongang bangus, tomato salsa, chili buro, cabinet-dried mango, dried ube powder, and mango roll — showcasing how Pangasinan's iconic produce can be transformed into premium packaged food products.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Technical assistance in developing new food products from Pangasinan's agri-fishery resources (bangus, onion, mango, etc.), including product formulation, sensory evaluation, and pilot-scale production
  • Equipment Access & Shared Processing Facilities — DOST-funded equipment including spray dryer, water retort, vacuum fryer, and freeze dryer, enabling MSMEs to produce shelf-stable, value-added products without investing in expensive machinery
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Compliance — Guidance on FDA product registration, food safety audits, and GMP compliance to help MSMEs meet national regulatory requirements
  • Packaging & Nutrition Labeling — Support for nutrition facts labeling, packaging design, and compliance with Philippine FDA food labeling standards
  • Consultancy & Technical Assistance — One-on-one consultancy for local food processors, MSMEs, and students on food technology applications and product improvement
  • MSME Training & Technology Transfer — Skills training on food processing methods, equipment operation, product quality control, and food innovation for Region I entrepreneurs and communities
