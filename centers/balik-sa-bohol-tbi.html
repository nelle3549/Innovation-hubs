BALIK sa Bohol TBI

Bohol Island State University · Region VII

BALIK sa Bohol TBI is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Bohol Island State University (BISU) in Tagbilaran City, Bohol, Region VII (Central Visayas). "BALIK" stands for Business Accelerators Linking Innovation and Knowledge, and the center serves as a platform for translating research and innovation into scalable, market-ready solutions while fostering collaboration among academe, industry, government, and local communities.

About the Center

Funded by the DOST-Philippine Council for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD) under the HEIRIT 2.0 program, the TBI supports startups, MSMEs, and technology-based enterprises across Bohol. In 2025, BISU BALIK sa Bohol TBI received the Rising TBI Award at the ReSEED Honors during the 9th Technology Business Incubation Summit, recognizing its growth and potential as an emerging innovation hub.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — Structured support guiding incubatees from early stage to market-ready enterprises.
  • Pre-Incubation & Ideation — Early guidance to help innovators validate and develop startup concepts.
  • Acceleration Services — Programs to help maturing startups and MSMEs scale their operations.
  • Mentoring & Advisory — Access to mentors, technical experts, and business advisors.
  • Technopreneurship Training — Capacity-building on business modeling, product development, and enterprise management.
  • DOST-PCIEERD Grant & Funding Linkage — Assistance in accessing DOST seed funding and startup grants.
  • Market & Network Linkage — Connections to markets, investors, industry, and the innovation ecosystem.
  • IP & Technology Transfer Support — Guidance on protecting and commercializing research and innovations.
