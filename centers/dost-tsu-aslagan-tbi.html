Technology Business Incubator

DOST-TSU Aslagan TBI

Tarlac State University · Region III

DOST-TSU Aslagan TBI is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Tarlac State University in Tarlac City, Region III (Central Luzon). Officially launched on July 24, 2023, the center takes its name "Aslagan," a Kapampangan term meaning "to light up," reflecting its mission to nurture entrepreneurs and innovators by providing a conducive environment and essential resources for building their businesses.

About the Center

Funded by the DOST-Philippine Council for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD) under the Higher Education Institution Readiness for Innovation and Technopreneurship (HEIRIT) program, the TBI is managed by TSU's Office of Technology Development, Transfer, and Commercialization. Its incubation work has a strong focus on the food industry, with laboratory testing supported by the Food Technology and Research Center at the TSU Lucinda Campus.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — Structured multi-month incubation supporting startups from ideation through to a market-ready enterprise.
  • Pre-Incubation & Ideation Support — Early-stage guidance to help aspiring technopreneurs validate and shape their business concepts.
  • Mentoring & Advisory — Access to industry mentors, technical experts, and business advisors throughout the incubation cycle.
  • Food Technology Laboratory Access — Product testing and development support through the TSU Food Technology and Research Center.
  • Technopreneurship Training — Capacity-building workshops on business modeling, product development, and enterprise management.
  • DOST-PCIEERD Grant & Funding Linkage — Assistance in accessing DOST seed funding and startup grant opportunities.
  • IP & Technology Transfer Support — Guidance on intellectual property protection and commercialization of innovations.
  • Market Linkage & Networking — Connections to markets, investors, and the broader innovation ecosystem.
