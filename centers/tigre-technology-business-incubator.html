TIGRE Technology Business Incubator (NCF)

Naga College Foundation (NCF) · Region V

TIGRE TBI is the Technology Business Incubator of Naga College Foundation (NCF) in Naga City, Camarines Sur. Supported by DOST, TIGRE TBI operates as NCF's official startup hub, committed to building a thriving technology entrepreneurship ecosystem in the Bicol Region. The center runs structured incubation cohort programs — including the Geronimo cohort and the NEXUS initiative in partnership with DTI — providing aspiring entrepreneurs with the tools, mentorship, and network needed to launch scalable tech-based and social enterprises.

About the Center

Naga College Foundation is a private institution in Naga City with a strong heritage in education. TIGRE TBI reflects the school's evolution toward innovation and entrepreneurship, channeling the energy of Bicol's young talent into ventures with commercial and social impact. The center maintains a dedicated Facebook page (facebook.com/ncftigretbi) and engages actively with the startup community, posting regular updates about cohort activities, partnerships, and milestones.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation Cohorts — Structured cohort programs (including the Geronimo cohort) that take startup teams from ideation through validated prototypes and early traction
  • Bootcamp & Training Programs — Intensive workshops on entrepreneurship, lean startup methodology, design thinking, and product-market fit
  • Mentorship Network — Access to NCF faculty, industry professionals, and government agency advisors supporting startup growth
  • Business Development Support — Guidance on business planning, pitching to investors, and navigating early-stage company operations
  • Social Enterprise Support — Incubation of technology-based social enterprises addressing community needs in the Bicol Region
  • Co-working Space — Shared innovation workspace on the NCF campus for resident teams and alumni startups
