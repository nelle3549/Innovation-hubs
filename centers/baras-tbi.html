Technology Business Incubator

BARAS TBI (Business Assistance for Research Acceleration & Sustainability)

Bulacan State University · Region III

BARAS TBI (Business Assistance for Research Acceleration & Sustainability) is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Bulacan State University (BulSU) in Malolos, Bulacan. Under DOST's banner program for Industrial Technology, BARAS TBI supports entrepreneurs and innovators in sectors including food processing, jewelry making, furniture design, and other industrial crafts indigenous to Central Luzon.

About the Center

Operating through BulSU's Office of the Vice President for Research, Extension and Innovation (OVPREI), BARAS TBI bridges university research capabilities with the needs of local entrepreneurs and MSMEs, transforming research outputs into commercially viable products and businesses.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Workspace, mentoring, and business development for industrial technology ventures
  • Technology Commercialization — Support for converting research and innovations into market-ready products
  • DOST Funding Access — Linkage to DOST-TAPI grants, TECHNICOM, and innovation funding
  • Business Planning & Development — Guidance on business modeling, IP management, and legal compliance
  • Mentoring — Access to BulSU faculty experts, industry mentors, and DOST TBI network coaches
  • MSME Support — Programs tailored to local food, jewelry, furniture, and handicraft entrepreneurs
  • Demo Days & Showcases — Events to present innovations to investors and potential partners
