DOST-PUP TBI Pylon Hub

Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) · NCR

The DOST-PUP TBI Pylon Hub — full name: PUP Technology Business Incubation Product Innovation Yielding to Long-Term Opportunities for New Entrepreneurs Hub — is the Technology Business Incubator of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) in Manila, serving the National Capital Region. Established through a grant from DOST-PCIEERD, Pylon Hub promotes innovation and technopreneurship at PUP, turning student and early-stage founder ideas into commercially viable businesses.

About the Center

The "PYLON" name reflects PUP's role as a structural pillar of the national innovation ecosystem — a hub that connects entrepreneurial talent at one of the Philippines' largest and most diverse state universities with the resources, mentorship, and networks needed to build scalable technology enterprises. Pylon Hub is active in Manila's startup community, participating in Pista ng Inobasyon (Innovation Festival) events and collaborating with other Manila-based tech hubs to strengthen the NCR startup ecosystem.

The center is particularly focused on supply chain and logistics innovation — reflecting PUP's strengths in engineering, business, and technology — alongside broader technology startup development. It runs an active annual cohort program and year-round events that showcase and accelerate PUP-based ventures.

Programs & Services

  • PYLON Innovators Incubation Program — A six-month structured incubation program for early-stage startups, delivering mentorship in Supply Chain Engineering, Logistics Simulation, Software Development, and Product-to-Startup Conversion
  • Co-working Space & Innovation Facilities — Access to co-working and incubation space at PUP Manila, providing incubatees with a collaborative environment and access to university resources and labs
  • IP Support & Technology Commercialization — Intellectual property advisory, patent filing support, and technology commercialization guidance to help founders protect and monetize their innovations
  • Funding Agency Engagement — Direct facilitation of connections to DOST-PCIEERD grants, startup financing programs, angel investors, and venture development opportunities for qualifying startups
  • Pista ng Inobasyon & Demo Events — Participation in PUP's annual Innovation Festival and regular startup showcase events, providing incubatees with public visibility and investor exposure
  • Cross-Hub Collaboration — Partnership with other Manila-based tech hubs and innovation centers to strengthen the NCR startup ecosystem and provide incubatees with expanded networking and co-creation opportunities
