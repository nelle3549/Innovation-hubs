Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab SLU — Saint Louis University

Saint Louis University (SLU-EISSIF) · CAR

FabLab SLU is the digital fabrication laboratory of Saint Louis University, operating under the SLU Engineering Institute and Shared Service Infrastructure Facilities (EISSIF) in Baguio City. Established through the DTI Shared Service Facilities (SSF) program, FabLab SLU is registered on the global fablabs.io directory and serves as SLU's hands-on innovation and prototyping facility in the Cordillera Administrative Region.

About the Center

Located in Baguio City — the educational and commercial hub of northern Philippines — FabLab SLU provides digital fabrication access to SLU students and the broader Baguio City innovation ecosystem. The lab operates in close proximity to the SIRIB Center (SLU Incubator for Research, Innovation & Business), creating an integrated dual-asset innovation campus where startup incubatees can prototype physical products using the lab's digital fabrication tools.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for product prototyping and model fabrication
  • Laser cutting and engraving for precision material processing
  • CNC milling/routing for component and part fabrication
  • Vinyl cutting for graphic and signage applications
  • CAD workstations for digital design and engineering
  • Open fabrication access for SLU students and faculty researchers
  • SIRIB Center integration — FabLab support for SLU-incubated hardware and product startups
  • Fabrication support for MSMEs and innovators in Baguio City and the Cordillera area
