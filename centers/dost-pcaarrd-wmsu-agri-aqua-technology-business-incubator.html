DOST-PCAARRD-WMSU Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubator (ATBI)

Western Mindanao State University · Region IX

The DOST-PCAARRD-WMSU Agriculture & Food TBI is hosted by Western Mindanao State University in Zamboanga City and serves the Zamboanga Peninsula (Region IX). Established under the DOST-PCAARRD national Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubation (ATBI) Program, WMSU-ATBI provides emerging technopreneurs in the region with food and agricultural technology incubation services — translating mature AANR innovations into commercially viable businesses.

About the Center

WMSU-ATBI actively participates in the DOST-PCAARRD RAISE (Regional Agri-Aqua Innovation System Enhancement) Program, which strengthens the regional ecosystem for agriculture and aquaculture technology commercialization. The center held its RAISE Incubatee Graduation Ceremony in March 2025, honoring its latest cohort of agripreneurs who completed the full incubation journey.

The center serves agri-aqua entrepreneurs across the Zamboanga Peninsula — covering key agricultural sectors including rice, corn, fisheries, seaweed, and food processing — helping incubatees develop business plans, improve and differentiate their products, and connect with markets and financing.

Programs & Services

  • Agri-Aqua Technology Incubation — Full incubation support for food and agricultural technology startups and MSMEs, from technology adoption through to commercial launch
  • Technical & Marketing Assistance — Expert guidance on production technologies, product development, branding, packaging, and market entry for agri-food enterprises
  • Capacity Building & Training — Workshops and seminars for entrepreneurs on business fundamentals, innovation management, quality standards, and regulatory compliance
  • Business Development Services — Business plan development, financial modeling, and advisory coaching throughout the incubation cycle
  • DOST Funding Facilitation — Connecting incubatees to PCAARRD grants, DOST startup programs, and other national funding mechanisms to sustain business growth
