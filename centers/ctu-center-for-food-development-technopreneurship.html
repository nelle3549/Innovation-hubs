Food Innovation Center

CTU Center for Food Development & Technopreneurship (IFSIT) (IC)

Cebu Technological University (CTU) · Region VII

The Center for Food Development & Technopreneurship (IFSIT — Institute of Food Science and Technopreneurship) at Cebu Technological University is an RDE institute under CTU's College of Technology — it is not the DOST Region VII Food Innovation Center (that is the Central Visayas FIC hosted at CIT University). It is a multidisciplinary platform that bridges food science research and entrepreneurship, delivering technical services and R&D support to food-sector MSMEs, processors, and entrepreneurs across Cebu and the broader Central Visayas region.

About the Center

Located at CTU's main campus in Cebu City, IFSIT provides access to professional food laboratory facilities and analytical equipment — making food product compliance and commercialization achievable for small-scale producers who would otherwise lack these resources. The center offers an integrated approach covering product development, safety testing, packaging guidance, and business training under one roof.

As part of the DOST FIC network, IFSIT contributes to developing safe, nutritious, and market-ready food products from Central Visayas' agricultural resources — nurturing a pipeline of food technopreneurs and enterprises capable of competing in national and international markets, with particular focus on the region's indigenous and traditional food products.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — formulation, process design, and prototype development for new or improved food products tailored for commercial launch
  • Food Safety & Quality Testing — microbiological, physicochemical, and sensory evaluation services to ensure product compliance and quality assurance
  • Packaging & Labeling Assistance — guidance on food packaging design, FDA label compliance, and shelf-life testing for market-ready products
  • Technopreneurship Training — entrepreneurship programs equipping food science students and MSMEs to commercialize food innovations and launch food enterprises
  • MSME Technical Assistance — process optimization, equipment operation training, and consulting services for small-scale food enterprises and processors
  • Regulatory Compliance Guidance — support for FDA registration, HACCP planning, and food safety certification requirements
  • Collaborative R&D Projects — joint research with industry and government partners on functional foods, indigenous Visayan ingredients, and value-added processing
