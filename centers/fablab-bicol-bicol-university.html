Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Bicol — Bicol University

Bicol University (BUCIT) · Region V

FabLab Bicol is the digital fabrication laboratory of Bicol University's College of Industrial Technology (BUCIT), located in Legazpi City, Albay in the Bicol Region (Region V). Listed on the global fablabs.io directory as one of the Philippines' internationally recognized fabrication laboratories, FabLab Bicol serves as Bicol University's hands-on innovation and prototyping hub.

About the Center

BUCIT's industrial technology focus gives FabLab Bicol a practical, production-oriented character: the center emphasizes hands-on application of digital fabrication technologies to real industrial and entrepreneurial challenges in the Bicol region's manufacturing, agricultural, and creative sectors. The lab serves engineering and technology students, faculty researchers, and MSME entrepreneurs across the region.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for industrial prototyping and product development
  • Laser cutting and engraving for precision fabrication
  • CNC milling/routing for parts and component manufacture
  • Vinyl cutting for graphic and labeling applications
  • CAD workstations for technical design and engineering drawing
  • Fabrication support for Bicol Region MSMEs and student innovation projects
  • Research collaboration with BUCIT faculty for industrial technology R&D
  • Open access to students, faculty researchers, and community innovators across the Bicol region
