DLSU Animo Labs — TBI & FabLab

De La Salle University (DLSU) · NCR

Animo Labs is the Technology Business Incubator (TBI) and digital fabrication laboratory (FabLab) of De La Salle University, established in partnership with DOST-PCIEERD. Located primarily at the DLSU Manila campus in Taft Avenue, Manila (with facilities also at the Laguna campus), Animo Labs serves as DLSU's flagship innovation and entrepreneurship center — supporting student and community innovators from idea validation to prototype development to market-ready startup.

About the Center

Animo Labs integrates prototyping capabilities with structured incubation programs, allowing innovators to build, test, and iterate on their products within a single support ecosystem. The center reflects DLSU's Lasallian mission of holistic education and social impact, channeling the university's engineering, business, and design expertise toward technology entrepreneurship and community innovation. The center's branded identity — "Animo Labs" from the DLSU battle cry "Animo La Salle" — signals its deep integration into the DLSU community and culture.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation (TBI) — Structured incubation program for technology-based ventures, with mentorship, business development support, and access to DOST-PCIEERD's national incubation network
  • Prototyping & Digital Fabrication — FabLab services including 3D printing, circuit fabrication/PCB prototyping, laser cutting, and woodworking for product prototype development
  • Product Development Support — End-to-end assistance from concept design through prototype iteration to commercialization-ready product development
  • Mentorship & Advisory — Business coaching, technical mentoring, and industry connections from DLSU faculty, alumni, and industry partners
  • Training & Workshops — Capacity-building sessions in entrepreneurship, design thinking, prototyping tools, and startup methodologies for students and community innovators
  • Multi-Campus Access — Innovation support and prototyping resources available at both the Manila and Laguna DLSU campuses
  • DOST-PCIEERD Program Access — Connections to national funding programs, the startup ecosystem network, and DOST incubation infrastructure for scale-up and commercialization support
