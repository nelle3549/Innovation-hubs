Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

Aklan Innovators Hub FabLab

Aklan State University (ASU) · Region VI

The Aklan Innovators Hub FabLab is the digital fabrication laboratory of Aklan State University (ASU) in Banga, Aklan. Established with a PHP 18.5 million investment as one of the DTI- and DOST-supported Fab Labs in Western Visayas, it serves as the fourth Fab Lab in the region — expanding access to advanced manufacturing and prototyping infrastructure for inventors, startups, students, and MSMEs across Aklan Province.

About the Center

Aklan State University, a state university mandated to provide higher education, research, and community extension services in Aklan, hosts the FabLab as part of its commitment to technology-driven development in Western Visayas. The center supports product innovation, R&D activities, and skills development, providing entrepreneurs and students with equipment that would otherwise be inaccessible to them individually.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing for product prototyping, scale models, and custom components
  • CNC Routing — Computer-controlled material removal for wood, foam, and soft composites
  • Prototyping & R&D Support — Technical assistance for inventors and MSME owners developing new products or improving existing ones
  • Innovation Training & Workshops — Hands-on capacity building for students and community members on digital fabrication and entrepreneurship
  • Startup Support Services — Collaboration space and equipment access for early-stage technology ventures in Aklan
