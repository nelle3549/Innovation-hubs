About the Center

FabLab CTU-Argao is a digital fabrication laboratory established through a partnership between Cebu Technological University – Argao Campus and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) under the University Shared Facility (USF) Program. Located in Argao, Cebu, the lab is housed within the CTU-Argao campus and provides open access to digital fabrication technologies for faculty, students, communities, and micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs).

The DTI-USF Program deploys Fab Labs at university campuses as a nationwide initiative to bridge the prototyping gap — enabling local innovators to move from concept to prototype faster and at lower cost than traditional manufacturing. CTU-Argao's FabLab serves as the anchor rapid prototyping and digital fabrication facility for the southern Cebu area.

Programs & Services

FabLab CTU-Argao provides open-access fabrication services to MSMEs, students, researchers, and the broader community:

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping for product development, concept models, and custom parts
  • Laser Cutting / Engraving — Precision cutting and surface engraving on wood, acrylic, and other materials
  • CNC Milling / Routing — Computer-controlled subtractive manufacturing for complex shapes and components
  • Vinyl Cutting — Signage, decals, stickers, and custom labels
  • Large-Format and UV Printing — High-resolution printing for displays, signage, and product packaging
  • CAD Workstations — Computer-aided design support for users developing digital designs for fabrication

Primary beneficiaries: MSMEs in Argao and southern Cebu requiring prototyping support; CTU-Argao students and faculty engaged in research and product development; community innovators and entrepreneurs.
