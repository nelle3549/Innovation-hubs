T.I.P. TechnoCoRe (Student Incubation)

Technological Institute of the Philippines (TIP) · NCR

The TechnoCoRe Catalyst Office is the in-house student and startup incubation arm of the Technological Institute of the Philippines (TIP) in Quezon City. Operating within TIP TechnoCoRe (Technopreneurship and Collaborative Applied Research), it houses the university's Innovation and Technology Support Office (ITSO) and technopreneurship programs, supporting TIP student and faculty innovators from idea development through intellectual property protection and startup formation.

About the Center

Its ITSO function — recognized by the Intellectual Property Office of the Philippines (IPOPHL) — drives IP awareness, patent search and drafting, and technology transfer, while its technopreneurship track helps innovators build market-ready ventures. The Catalyst Office is distinct from TIP NITRO, the university's separate DOST-partnership technology business incubator.

Contact: technocore.tip.edu.ph; itso.technocore@tip.edu.ph / technocore@tip.edu.ph; (+632) 8911 0964 ext. 550.

Programs & Services

  • Student & startup incubation — Guides TIP student and faculty innovators in turning ideas into market-ready ventures.
  • Innovation and Technology Support Office (ITSO) — IPOPHL-recognized office providing IP awareness seminars and training in patent search and drafting.
  • Intellectual property & technology transfer — Support for protecting and commercializing inventions by TIP student and faculty innovators.
  • Technopreneurship programs — Enterprise-development and collaborative applied research linking academe with industry.
  • Industry & partner collaboration — Partnerships with industry players (e.g., Siemens, AboitizPower) advancing applied research and digital transformation.
  • Mentoring & capacity-building — Training and mentorship for innovators developing socially relevant, disruptive solutions.
