Palawan International Technology Business Incubator (PITBI)

Palawan State University · Region IV-B

The Palawan International Technology Business Incubator (PITBI) is the Technology Business Incubator (TBI) of Palawan State University (PSU), located in Puerto Princesa City, Palawan. Established with support from DOST, PITBI is one of the few TBIs in the country with an explicitly international orientation — designed to connect Palawan-based startups, researchers, and innovators with global markets, international funding, and cross-border technology partnerships.

About the Center

Palawan State University is the premier state university in the island province of Palawan, known for its rich biodiversity, marine resources, and ecotourism industry. PITBI aligns its incubation work with Palawan's unique development context, supporting technology ventures in areas such as eco-friendly product development, marine and coastal resources, sustainable agriculture, tourism technology, and natural resources management. The center provides a nurturing environment for student and community entrepreneurs seeking to launch scalable, technology-driven enterprises with both local and global relevance.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Full-cycle incubation for early-stage technology startups with global market potential, from ideation through commercialization
  • International Linkage Program — Connections to international incubators, innovation networks, and cross-border funding opportunities aligned with PITBI's international mandate
  • Technology Mentorship — Guidance from PSU faculty, industry professionals, and international advisors on technology development and market entry
  • Business Development Support — Coaching on business model design, market validation, financial planning, and pitch preparation
  • DOST Funding Connections — Access to DOST grants, seed funding programs, and the national TBI network for commercialization support
  • IP Management Support — Assistance with patent applications, trade secret protection, and technology licensing
  • Co-working & Innovation Space — Dedicated workspace on the PSU campus for resident startup teams
  • Sustainability & Ecotourism Tech Focus — Specialized support for ventures in Palawan's key sectors: ecotourism, marine/coastal resources, sustainable agriculture, and biodiversity conservation technology
