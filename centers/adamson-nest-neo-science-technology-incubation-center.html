Adamson NEST — Neo Science & Technology Incubation Center

Adamson University · NCR

NEST (Neo Science and Technology Incubation Center) — formally incorporated as AdUNEST Inc. — is the Technology Business Incubator of Adamson University in Manila, serving the National Capital Region. NEST is part of the SCALE-NCR (Strategic and Collaborative Alliance for Leveraging the Ecosystem of Startups – NCR) consortium, a DOST-NCR-supported network of Metro Manila TBIs that includes TIP NITRO (lead), DLSU Animo Labs, UST TOMASInno Center, Mapua Think and Tinker Lab, UPD UPSCALE Innovation Hub, MC TBI, and QBO Innovation Hub.

About the Center

NEST's mission sets it apart in the NCR TBI landscape: it focuses on building technology-based startups and social innovations that are socially, environmentally, and economically sensitive to the needs of the marginalized, nature, and broader society. Embedded within Adamson University's Vincentian tradition of service to the poor and commitment to sustainable development, NEST nurtures ideas from both the academe and industry that go beyond commercial viability to create genuine social value.

As AdUNEST Inc. — a formally incorporated non-stock, non-profit organization — NEST operates with the capacity to formally engage government agencies, private sector partners, and DOST programs in building a startup community aligned with Adamson's values of sustainability, innovation, and compassionate entrepreneurship.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation Program — End-to-end incubation support for early-stage technology-based startups at Adamson University, emphasizing socially and environmentally conscious innovations that address real community needs
  • Social Innovation Development — Dedicated programming for ventures focused on inclusive economic growth, environmental sustainability, and solutions for marginalized communities — reflecting AdUNEST's distinctive Vincentian social mission
  • SCALE-NCR Consortium Access — As a member of the DOST-NCR SCALE-NCR consortium, NEST provides startups with access to the broader Metro Manila innovation ecosystem including mentors, resources, and programs from across all eight partner TBIs
  • Business Development & Mentorship — Coaching on business model design, pitch preparation, financial modeling, and market entry strategy with guidance from Adamson faculty advisors and industry mentors
  • DOST-NCR Funding Access — Gateway to DOST-NCR startup grants and SCALE-NCR funding programs for qualifying socially-oriented technology-based enterprises incubated at NEST
