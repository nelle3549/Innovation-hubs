UCIANS — UC Innovation & Nurturing Space (TBI)

University of the Cordilleras (UC) · CAR

UCIANS (UC Innovation & Nurturing Space) is the Technology Business Incubator and innovation hub of the University of the Cordilleras (UC) in Baguio City, operating under the University's Innovation, Technology Transfer, and Entrepreneurship Office (InTTO). UCIANS serves as the primary platform for cultivating startups, student entrepreneurs, and technology ventures at UC — translating the university's academic research and student innovation into commercially viable businesses that contribute to the Cordillera region's economy.

About the Center

Baguio City, as a major urban center in the Cordillera highlands, has a growing startup culture driven by its concentration of universities, the BPO sector, and a creative professional community. UCIANS taps into this environment, providing UC's students, faculty, and community innovators with mentorship, workspace, and DOST-linked programs needed to develop startups from ideation through commercialization. The center's website (intto.uc-bcf.edu.ph) and InTTO Facebook presence document its innovation activities and programs.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Structured incubation support for technology-based and creative startup teams from UC's student and faculty community, covering ideation, validation, prototyping, and early commercialization
  • Technology Transfer Support — Facilitation of intellectual property filing, technology licensing, and commercialization of research outputs from UC faculty and graduate research
  • Entrepreneurship Training — Workshops and programs on business model development, market research, financial planning, and pitch preparation for UC student entrepreneurs
  • Mentorship & Expert Coaching — Access to UC faculty experts, industry practitioners, and InTTO network mentors for startup development and business strategy
  • DOST Program Linkage — Connections to DOST startup grants, PCIEERD incubation programs, and regional funding opportunities for qualifying UC startup ventures
  • Innovation Community Events — Bootcamps, demo days, and community activities connecting UC innovators, alumni entrepreneurs, and the wider Baguio startup ecosystem
  • IP & Legal Guidance — Advisory support on intellectual property protection, technology licensing agreements, and regulatory compliance for startup ventures
