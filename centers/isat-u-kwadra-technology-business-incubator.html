ISAT-U KWADRA Technology Business Incubator

Iloilo Science and Technology University (ISAT-U) · Region VI

KWADRA TBI is the Technology Business Incubator of Iloilo Science and Technology University (ISAT-U) in Iloilo City, supported by the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) under the HEIRIT (Higher Education Institution Readiness for Innovation & Technopreneurship) Program. Operating as one of Western Visayas' key DOST-accredited TBIs, KWADRA provides a structured innovation and startup incubation environment for students, faculty innovators, and community entrepreneurs in the Iloilo area.

About the Center

ISAT-U is a state university with strong programs in science, engineering, technology, and fisheries, and serves as a leading institution in Region VI's research and innovation ecosystem. KWADRA TBI harnesses the university's technical expertise to support product development and commercialization, particularly in agri-fishery technology, engineering solutions, and ICT ventures relevant to Western Visayas. The center maintains a dedicated website (kwadratbi.tech) and Facebook page (facebook.com/KwadraTBI) for its community of incubatees and partners.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — End-to-end incubation for tech-based startup teams from ideation through early market launch
  • Mentorship & Coaching — Guidance from ISAT-U faculty, industry professionals, and DOST-accredited mentors on technology development and business strategy
  • Prototyping Support — Access to university laboratory facilities and equipment to support prototype development and testing
  • Business Development — Coaching on business model canvas, market validation, financial planning, and investor pitch preparation
  • DOST HEIRIT Program Access — Participation in the national DOST Higher Education Institution Readiness for Innovation & Technopreneurship framework, including funding and network opportunities
  • IP & Technology Commercialization — Support for intellectual property protection and commercialization of ISAT-U-developed technologies
  • Co-working & Innovation Space — Dedicated incubation space on the ISAT-U campus for resident startup teams
  • Demo Days & Industry Linkage — Showcase events connecting incubatees with investors, industry partners, and potential customers in Western Visayas
