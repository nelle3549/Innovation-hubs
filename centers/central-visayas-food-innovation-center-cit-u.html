Food Innovation Center

Central Visayas Food Innovation Center — CIT-U

Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CIT-U) · Region VII

The Central Visayas Food Innovation Center (CVFIC) is the region's DOST-designated Food Innovation Center (FIC), hosted by Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CIT-U) in Cebu City. As one of the nationwide network of DOST FICs, the CVFIC provides comprehensive food product development and processing support to micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) across the Central Visayas region.

About the Center

Equipped with the standard DOST FIC machinery suite — including spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer — the CVFIC enables food entrepreneurs and producers in Cebu, Bohol, Siquijor, and Negros Oriental to develop, process, and commercialize food products to quality and regulatory standards.

Programs & Services

  • Food product development and formulation support for MSMEs
  • Food processing using spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer
  • Food safety and regulatory compliance guidance (FDA registration, Halal and organic certification pathways)
  • Packaging design and nutrition labeling assistance
  • Product costing and market feasibility analysis
  • Food product testing and quality assurance
  • Equipment rental for processing runs
  • MSME training and capacity-building in food technology and product development
