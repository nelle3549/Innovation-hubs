MMSU Food Innovation Center

Mariano Marcos State University (MMSU) · Region I

The MMSU Food Innovation Center (MMSU FIC) is the DOST-supported Food Innovation Center for Region I (Ilocos Region), hosted by Mariano Marcos State University (MMSU) in Batac City, Ilocos Norte. Established as part of DOST’s nationwide Food Innovation Center program, the MMSU FIC serves as a one-stop shop for food product development, food processing technology, and MSME support across the Ilocos region — helping local agri-food producers transform raw agricultural commodities into commercially viable, market-ready food products.

About the Center

MMSU, as the premier state university and Startupreneur anchor institution of Ilocos Norte, is strategically positioned to host the regional FIC. The university’s programs in agriculture, food technology, and engineering provide the academic and technical foundation for the FIC’s R&D and extension work, while its deep community ties to the Ilocos farming sector make it the natural hub for food innovation serving the region’s iconic products — Ilocos garlic, Vigan longganisa, bagnet, pinakbet, vinegar, and other agri-food specialties.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Technical assistance in developing and improving food products from Region I’s agricultural commodities, including Ilocos garlic, Vigan longganisa, bagnet, vinegar, and other regional specialties with high commercialization potential
  • Food Processing Technology Services — Access to DOST-standard food processing equipment including spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer for pilot-scale production and food technology application
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Assistance — Guidance on FDA product registration, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), HACCP implementation, and food safety compliance for Region I’s food MSMEs and cooperatives
  • Packaging & Nutrition Labeling — Technical support for consumer-ready packaging design, nutrition facts labeling, and FDA-compliant food label development to help Ilocos products reach retail and export markets
  • Product Costing & Market Access — Business development support covering production cost computation, pricing strategies, and market linkage for food entrepreneurs entering regional and national distribution channels
  • MSME Training & Capacity Building — Training workshops and hands-on programs for food entrepreneurs, farmers, cooperatives, and processors on food technology, GMP, and DOST SETUP programs across Region I
  • Technology Extension & Advisory — Dissemination of DOST-developed food technologies and technical advisory services for agri-food enterprises across Ilocos Norte, Ilocos Sur, Abra, and neighboring provinces
