Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

Capiz Innovation Center FabLab

Provincial Govt of Capiz · Region VI

The Capiz Innovation Center FabLab is a fabrication laboratory operated by the Provincial Government of Capiz in Roxas City, Capiz. It is the province's dedicated digital fabrication and prototyping facility, providing Capiz residents, entrepreneurs, students, and MSMEs with access to digital manufacturing equipment and creative technology tools. The center is active at facebook.com/capizinnovate and can be reached at (036) 520-2584 or capizinnovate@gmail.com for service appointments and inquiries.

About the Center

The center serves as key innovation infrastructure for Capiz province — the "Seafood Capital of the Philippines" — supporting the creative and manufacturing sectors of the wider Western Visayas region. Its digital fabrication services are used for product prototyping, custom component production, business signage, laser engraving commissions, and artisanal design work, fostering a local innovation culture across Capiz's entrepreneurial community and complementing the broader DOST innovation ecosystem in Region VI.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing for product prototypes, models, custom parts, and design mockups for Capiz-based entrepreneurs and students
  • Laser Engraving & Cutting — Precision laser engraving and cutting for wood, acrylic, leather, and similar materials — including active commercial laser-engraving jobs
  • CNC Fabrication — CNC-controlled cutting and milling for precision parts and custom product components
  • Product Prototyping Support — End-to-end prototyping assistance from design to physical prototype for local innovators and MSMEs
  • Creative Industry Services — Digital fabrication support for artists, craftspeople, and the local creative economy in Roxas City and Capiz province
  • By-Appointment Access — Open to Capiz residents and enterprises; appointments via facebook.com/capizinnovate or (036) 520-2584
