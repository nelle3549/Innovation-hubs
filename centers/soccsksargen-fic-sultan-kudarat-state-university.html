Food Innovation Center

SOCCSKSARGEN FIC — Sultan Kudarat State University

Sultan Kudarat State University · Region XII

The SOCCSKSARGEN Food Innovation Center (FIC) at Sultan Kudarat State University (SKSU) is the first DOST Food Innovation Center in the SOCCSKSARGEN region (Region XII), established in 2022 and hosted by SKSU in Tacurong City, Sultan Kudarat. The center serves food MSMEs and agri-food entrepreneurs of SOCCSKSARGEN — a region known for its pineapple, banana, coffee, and corn production — with food product development, processing, and packaging services.

About the Center

As part of the nationwide DOST FIC network, the SKSU FIC provides MSMEs in SOCCSKSARGEN with access to specialized food processing equipment, technical expertise, and regulatory guidance that would otherwise require travel to major urban centers. The center works closely with DOST Region XII (DOST-12) to support the region's agri-food value chain and build the food processing capacity of local enterprises.

Sultan Kudarat State University's strong foundation in agricultural and food sciences makes it a natural host for the region's first FIC, bridging the university's research capabilities with practical MSME development needs across SOCCSKSARGEN.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Technical assistance for MSMEs developing new food products, from concept to market-ready formulation
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Assistance — Guidance on FDA registration, food safety compliance, and standards for local and export markets
  • Packaging & Nutrition Labeling — Support for label design, nutrition facts computation, and packaging specification
  • Product Costing & Pricing — Assistance with cost of production analysis and pricing strategy for food products
  • Product Testing & Quality Assurance — Access to food testing facilities for physical, chemical, and microbiological analysis
  • Equipment Rental — Access to FIC's food processing equipment including spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer
  • MSME Training — Capacity-building seminars and workshops on food processing technology, hygiene, and entrepreneurship for SOCCSKSARGEN food entrepreneurs
