WMSU Advanced Manufacturing Center (AMCen) / RICME (IC)

Western Mindanao State University (WMSU) · Region IX

The WMSU Advanced Manufacturing Center (AMCen) — co-housed with the Western Mindanao State University Regional Innovation Center for Metals and Engineering (RICME) — is the first Advanced Manufacturing Center in the Zamboanga Peninsula and the ninth nationwide, officially launched in September 2025 through a partnership between DOST-MIRDC, DOST Region IX, and Western Mindanao State University in Zamboanga City. The center brings cutting-edge 3D printing and additive manufacturing technology to the ZamPen region for the first time, with RICME providing its permanent institutional home within WMSU.

About the Center

The AMCen provides WMSU students, researchers, and industry partners in ZamPen with direct access to advanced rapid prototyping tools — initially using polymer-based 3D printing, with a roadmap to expand into metal-based additive manufacturing in partnership with DOST-MIRDC's central office in Taguig. WMSU students are already using the center's 3D printers for thesis projects and prototype designs. Technology licensing agreements with DOST-ITDI further expand the center's capacity to commercialize WMSU innovations in manufacturing.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing / Rapid Prototyping — FDM polymer 3D printing for thesis prototypes, product models, and custom component parts for WMSU students, researchers, and industry partners
  • Metal-Based Additive Manufacturing (Planned) — Expansion into metal 3D printing in partnership with DOST-MIRDC Taguig for industrial-grade metal prototyping
  • Engineering R&D Support — Metals and engineering research support for WMSU faculty and student projects under the DOST-MIRDC national MEIC and AMCen program
  • MSME Innovation Assistance — Open-access manufacturing services for ZamPen enterprises adopting additive manufacturing and seeking production efficiency improvements
  • Technology Licensing — Facilitation of technology licensing agreements with DOST-ITDI for commercializing innovations developed at the WMSU RICME/AMCen
  • By-Appointment Fabrication Access — Equipment scheduling through the RICME Facebook page for innovators, researchers, and industry partners across Zamboanga Peninsula
