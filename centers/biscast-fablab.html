Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

BISCAST FabLab

Bicol State College of Applied Sciences & Technology · Region V

The BISCAST FabLab (also known as BISCAST ManFabLab) is the digital fabrication laboratory of Bicol State College of Applied Sciences and Technology (BISCAST) in Naga City, Camarines Sur. As part of the nationwide Fab Lab network, the center provides access to advanced digital fabrication and prototyping tools for students, MSMEs, and community innovators in the Bicol Region.

About the Center

BISCAST, a specialized CHED-recognized state college focused on applied sciences and technology, hosts the FabLab as a key component of its technology-transfer and community-service programs. The center is registered in the global fablabs.io directory under the name "biscastmanfablab," reflecting its identity as a manufacturing and fabrication hub within the institution. With a dedicated email contact (biscast.manfablab@gmail.com), the FabLab operates as a distinct unit serving external clients and partner communities alongside its academic programs.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Prototyping of products, models, and custom components via fused deposition modeling (FDM)
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — High-precision cutting and marking on acrylic, wood, leather, and fabric
  • CNC Milling & Routing — Computer-controlled machining of wood, foam, and composite materials
  • CAD Workstations — Design software access for engineering, architecture, and product development students and professionals
  • Fabrication Support for MSMEs — Assistance for local businesses in developing new products and improving packaging through digital manufacturing
