Upgrade Innolab, Inc. (formerly UP Mindanao UPGRADE TBI)

Independent (private non-profit; spun off from UP Mindanao in 2022) · Region XI

UPGRADE (University of the Philippines Mindanao Growing and Developing Enterprises) is the Technology Business Incubator hosted by the University of the Philippines Mindanao in Davao City, serving Region XI (Davao Region). Founded in 2019 through a grant from DOST-PCIEERD under the HEIRIT program, UPGRADE launched as UP Mindanao's dedicated incubation hub with a niche program centered on ICT for Sustainable Food Systems — one of the few university TBIs in the Philippines focused on the intersection of digital technology and food system innovation.

About the Center

In November 2022, UPGRADE transitioned from a university-based TBI to an independent private organization — UPGRADE Innolab, Inc. — incorporated with the Securities and Exchange Commission as a non-stock, non-profit entity. This transition positioned UPGRADE to scale its reach and serve a broader ecosystem of founders beyond the UP Mindanao campus while maintaining its deep roots in Davao and Mindanao's innovation community. UPGRADE continues to serve as a guide institution for DOST-PCIEERD's HEIRIT 2.0 program universities in Mindanao, supporting newly established TBIs across the island group.

In its first three years as a private organization, UPGRADE Innolab incubated 60 startups and MSMEs, facilitated more than ₱60 million in funding for its portfolio companies, and supported over 3,500 job opportunities across its ventures and partner network — making it one of the most impactful incubators in Mindanao.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation Program — Core incubation support for scalable, technology-based startups in sustainable food systems, food products, bio-economy, smart industries, and green economy solutions, with structured mentorship from industry experts and DOST advisors
  • IDEAS Davao — A regional innovation and entrepreneurship activation program connecting early-stage startups and aspiring founders in Davao City to UPGRADE's network, resources, and incubation pipeline
  • Business Development & Mentorship — Coaching on business model design, product-market fit validation, pitch preparation, market entry strategy, and financial planning for portfolio startups
  • Access to Funding — Facilitation of ₱60M+ in startup financing through connections to DOST grants, angel investors, venture networks, and government-backed funding programs
  • Mindanao TBI Network — As guide institution for HEIRIT 2.0, UPGRADE supports and strengthens newly established TBIs across Mindanao, fostering a collective innovation ecosystem and regional knowledge-sharing among Mindanao incubators
