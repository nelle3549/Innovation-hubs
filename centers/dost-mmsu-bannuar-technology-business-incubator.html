DOST-MMSU Bannuar Technology Business Incubator

Mariano Marcos State University · Region I

The DOST-MMSU Technology Business Incubator — known as the Bannuar TBI — is hosted by Mariano Marcos State University in Batac, Ilocos Norte and serves Region I (Ilocos). Established through a ₱12.7 million grant from DOST-PCIEERD, the Bannuar TBI was inaugurated at MMSU to spur innovation and empower the creative manufacturing industry in the Ilocos Region. "Bannuar" is an Ilocano word meaning excellent or distinguished, reflecting the TBI's aspiration to be a premier incubation center in Northern Luzon.

About the Center

The Bannuar TBI supports startups and MSMEs in technology-driven sectors, with a particular emphasis on creative industries, manufacturing innovation, and technology commercialization. MMSU also operates a separate DOST-PCAARRD Agri-Aqua TBI focused on agricultural and food technology incubation, making it one of the few Philippine universities hosting multiple distinct TBI programs.

The TBI envisions nurturing innovation and entrepreneurship skills among students, faculty, and local communities in the Ilocos Region, supporting the launch and growth of technology-based ventures that use indigenous resources and improve business operations and competitiveness of regional MSMEs.

Programs & Services

  • Creative Manufacturing & Technology Incubation — Startup incubation for technology-driven enterprises in creative manufacturing, product design, ICT applications, and emerging technology sectors in the Ilocos Region
  • MSME Business Operations Improvement — Technical and advisory support for regional MSMEs seeking to adopt better practices, meet regulatory requirements, and commercialize technology-based products
  • R&D Technology Commercialization — Facilitation of technology transfer from MMSU research outputs to commercial enterprises, promoting the use of university-developed innovations in the regional economy
  • Employment & Enterprise Creation — Programs oriented toward job creation and new enterprise formation in the Ilocos Region through technopreneurship education and business development support
  • Business Development & Mentorship — Coaching on business planning, product development, market entry, and financial modeling for incubatee startups and early-stage technology enterprises
  • DOST Funding Access — Assistance in accessing DOST-PCIEERD grants, startup support programs, and national innovation funding for qualifying incubatee ventures
