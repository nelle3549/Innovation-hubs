About the Center

The SIRIB Center (SLU Incubator for Research, Innovation & Business) is Saint Louis University's technology business incubator and the primary innovation support platform for startups and research-based ventures in the Cordillera Administrative Region. Based at SLU's campus in Baguio City, the SIRIB Center operates under the banner of Smart Mountain Engineering Solutions — a vision that anchors the center's work in highland engineering, sustainable technology, and the unique innovation landscape of the Cordillera.

Founded in 2017 with a CHED grant (with DOST and DTI support), the SIRIB Center supports SLU student entrepreneurs, early-stage startups, and faculty researchers in developing ideas into viable technology ventures. Baguio City's profile as a highland center of education, IT services, and tourism creates a distinctive innovation context: the center's incubatees draw on the city's IT-BPM ecosystem, highland agricultural value chains, indigenous cultural assets, and the Cordillera's growing reputation as a hub for science and technology education in northern Philippines.

The SIRIB Center operates in proximity to FabLab SLU — giving incubatees integrated access to digital fabrication capabilities (3D printing, laser cutting, CNC machining) alongside business incubation services, making SLU a compact dual-asset innovation campus in the Cordillera.

Programs & Services

  • Startup incubation for early-stage ventures with focus on technology, engineering, and research-based innovation in the Cordillera context
  • Smart Mountain Engineering Solutions programs aligning innovation with highland agriculture, indigenous technology, IT services, and sustainable highland engineering
  • Mentoring and business development support from SLU faculty, industry practitioners, and DOST-PCIEERD partner mentors
  • IP and legal guidance for university-based researchers and student innovators commercializing research outputs
  • Access to DOST-PCIEERD funding and technology transfer programs through the national TBI accreditation network
  • FabLab SLU integration — direct linkage to digital fabrication tools for rapid prototyping and hardware startup development
  • CAR ecosystem connectivity — engagement with Baguio City's IT-BPM community, highland agricultural MSMEs, and local government innovation and entrepreneurship programs
