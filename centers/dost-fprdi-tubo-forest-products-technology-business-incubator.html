DOST-FPRDI Tubo Forest Products Technology Business Incubator (FPTBI)

Forest Products R&D Institute (DOST) · Region IV-A

The DOST-FPRDI Technology Business Incubator — officially rebranded as Tubò in September 2023 — is hosted by the DOST Forest Products Research and Development Institute (DOST-FPRDI) on the campus of the University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB) in Los Baños, Laguna. FPRDI was founded in 1954 as the Forest Products Laboratory and has since grown into the country's premier research institute for the forest products sector.

About the Center

Tubò (FPRDI's TBI) specializes in supporting startups and MSMEs in the forest-based sector — covering wood and non-wood forest products, biomaterials, and forest-derived specialty products. The TBI provides much-needed technical and business support services that help entrepreneurs translate forest products technologies into viable commercial enterprises, from business registration through to product certification and market entry.

The center serves both Laguna-based and national forest-product entrepreneurs, offering access to FPRDI's extensive testing laboratories, processing demonstration facilities, and pool of expert researchers. Partners have included Laguna State Polytechnic University (LSPU), regional farm enterprises, and a growing network of forest-based MSMEs accessing FPRDI innovations.

Programs & Services

  • Wood Products Processing Technology Transfer — Technical training and enterprise support for startups using wood processing technologies developed by FPRDI, including furniture, construction materials, and specialty wood products
  • Non-Wood Forest Products Incubation — Support for enterprises based on non-timber forest products such as bamboo, rattan, natural antimicrobials, essential oils, and other biomass-derived products
  • Technology Testing & Certification Assistance — Access to FPRDI's accredited testing laboratories for product quality testing, materials certification, and standards compliance required for market entry
  • Business Registration & Grant Application Support — Guided assistance for incubatees in completing business registration, applying for DOST grants, and navigating government permitting requirements
  • IP Protection Services — Intellectual property advisory and filing support to help forest-based innovators protect their product formulations, processes, and technologies
  • Business Mentoring on Finance & Marketing — Coaching on financial management, costing, pricing, marketing strategy, and business growth planning tailored to forest-product enterprises
  • Investor & Market Linkage — Facilitation of connections between incubatees and potential investors, buyers, distributors, and industry networks in the forest products and biomaterials sector
