Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab PFFI

Philippine Footwear Federation, Inc. (PFFI) · NCR

FabLab PFFI is the industry-embedded fabrication laboratory of the Philippine Footwear Federation Inc. (PFFI), located in Marikina City — the undisputed shoe capital of the Philippines and the heart of the country's footwear manufacturing industry. Unlike most Philippine FabLabs hosted by universities or state colleges, FabLab PFFI is operated by an industry association, making it a uniquely sector-focused fabrication facility designed to serve Marikina's footwear ecosystem.

About the Center

The center provides digital fabrication tools and technologies to footwear MSMEs in Marikina and the NCR, supporting product innovation, rapid prototyping of shoe designs and components, and the adoption of modern manufacturing techniques within a traditionally craft-based industry. FabLab PFFI represents a deliberate convergence of the global FabLab movement with the Philippines' most iconic local industry.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for rapid prototyping of footwear components, lasts, and sole designs
  • Laser cutting and engraving for precision cutting of leather, synthetic materials, and shoe patterns
  • CNC milling/routing for prototype molds and shoe component fabrication
  • Vinyl cutting for footwear patterns, branding, and trim applications
  • Fabrication support tailored to footwear MSME needs in Marikina's manufacturing ecosystem
  • Digital manufacturing tools to support modernization of traditional Philippine shoemaking
  • Open access to PFFI member enterprises and the broader Marikina footwear community
