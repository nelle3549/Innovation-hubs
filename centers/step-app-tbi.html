STEP APP — Sustainable Technopreneurship Ecosystem for the Promotion and Advancement of Promising Products — is the Technology Business Incubator hosted by Sultan Kudarat State University at its ACCESS Campus in Tacurong City, serving Region XII (SOCCSKSARGEN). It champions local startups in Industrial Technology and ICT for the Agriculture, Aquaculture, and Halal industries.

About the Center

The incubator now operates primarily under the STEP APP brand, with its own website at stepapptbi.com and its own Facebook presence. The former DOST-PCAARRD-SKSU Agri-Aqua TBI identity has been absorbed into it.

Recent activity is well documented: recognition by DEPDev as SKSU's innovation leader in Region 12 (August 2025), a blockchain and AI workshop, the STEPBuiltUP onboarding cohort, and benchmarking visits to CDO BITES. Its flagship success story is HOMI, a smart hotel management platform founded by Gabriel Jon Icawalo, which has secured deployment contracts with nearly 20 hotels.

Programs & Services

  • MOVE IT Incubation Program — the flagship framework (Maximizing Output Value Through Engaging and Innovative Technopreneurship), structured in three phases: WALK, JOG, and RUN.
  • WALK — Pre-incubation (4–9 months) — idea-shaping for potential entrepreneurs, with free use of the TBI facility. Activities include Call for Startup, reverse pitching, hackathons, roadshows, TechVangelization, and curriculum integration through capstone, thesis, and OJT.
  • JOG — Incubation (within one year) — entry requires a Business Plan approved by the Selection Panel. Mandatory modules cover incubation assessment and mentor matching, the Business Model Canvas, technology innovation and product development (agile, MVP, UX, and halal processes), and legal and regulatory considerations including IP protection and data privacy.
  • JOG — Elective modules — scaling technology ventures, funding and investment, marketing tech products, and ethics, social responsibility, and cultural sensitivity.
  • RUN — Post-incubation — the startup operates independently while the TBI continues support: royalty and client contracts, monitoring and evaluation, investors' forum, exhibits and trade fairs, business registration, IP protection, scale-up, and return service.
  • Halal-focused technical support — halal processes and practices are embedded as a formal unit within product development, and halal-industry legal considerations within the compliance module.
  • Partner network — DICT, DOST, DTI, Wadhwani Foundation, Provincial Government of Sultan Kudarat, LGU Tacurong, LGU Isulan, Innovate 360, BluC, and SICAD-SOX.
