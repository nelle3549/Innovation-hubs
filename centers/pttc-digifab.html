Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

PTTC DigiFab

Philippine Trade Training Center (DTI) · NCR

PTTC DigiFab is the digital fabrication facility of the Philippine Trade Training Center (PTTC), the training arm of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), located in Pasay City, Metro Manila. As a government-run Fab Lab under a major trade and industry agency, PTTC DigiFab uniquely targets MSMEs and entrepreneurs seeking to bridge the gap between product ideation and market-ready production — positioning it as one of the most commercially oriented Fab Labs in the National Capital Region.

About the Center

PTTC is mandated by DTI to develop the competitiveness of Philippine exporters, entrepreneurs, and MSMEs through training, standards, and technical assistance. DigiFab extends this mandate into the digital manufacturing space, giving entrepreneurs hands-on access to prototyping tools. The center has a dedicated official email (digifab@pttc.gov.ph) and website (pttc.gov.ph/digifab/), and is listed in the global fablabs.io network under "PTTCDIGIFAB."

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Prototype development for MSME products, packaging, and components using fused deposition and other additive manufacturing methods
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — High-precision cutting of acrylic, wood, and similar materials for product and display applications
  • CNC Milling & Routing — Subtractive fabrication for product molds, wooden fixtures, and material processing
  • Vinyl Cutting — Label, branding, and packaging prototype production for export-ready MSME products
  • CAD Workstations — Design software access for product engineers and MSME designers
  • MSME Business Assistance — DTI-backed technical and business development support integrated with fabrication services, including connections to export promotion programs and PTTC's broader MSME training curriculum
