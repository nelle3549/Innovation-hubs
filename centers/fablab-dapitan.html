Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Dapitan

Jose Rizal Memorial State University (JRMSU) · Region IX

FabLab Dapitan is the digital fabrication laboratory of Jose Rizal Memorial State University (JRMSU) in Dapitan City, Zamboanga del Norte. Part of the global Fab Lab network and the nationwide DOST/DTI-supported fabrication infrastructure in the Philippines, FabLab Dapitan brings advanced digital manufacturing tools to Western Mindanao, serving students, MSMEs, and innovators in the Zamboanga Peninsula (Region IX).

About the Center

JRMSU is a state university that takes its name from national hero Jose Rizal, who was exiled in Dapitan — a city of deep historical and cultural significance. The FabLab extends the university's mandate of serving its community and region by providing access to digital prototyping technology that enables local entrepreneurs and students to develop products, improve packaging, and build maker skills. The center is listed in the global fablabs.io directory under "fablabdapitan."

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of product designs, replacement parts, and custom models using FDM technology
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision cutting and surface marking of acrylic, wood, and other sheet materials for product and signage applications
  • CNC Milling & Routing — Computer-controlled material machining for wood, foam, and composite substrates
  • Vinyl Cutting — Production of decals, labels, and stickers for MSME branding and packaging
  • 3D Scanning — Digitization of physical objects for reverse engineering and product replication
  • Large-Format & UV Printing — Wide-format printing for display materials, banners, and product presentation
  • CAD Workstations — Design software access for students and entrepreneurs creating product drawings and 3D models
  • MSME Support & Prototyping — Technical assistance for Zamboanga Peninsula entrepreneurs developing new products or improving existing ones for market
