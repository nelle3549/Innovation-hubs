WVSU BINHI Technology Business Incubator (TBI)

West Visayas State University (WVSU) · Region VI

WVSU BINHI — Business Incubator Nurturing Homegrown Innovations — is the Technology Business Incubator (TBI) of West Visayas State University in La Paz, Iloilo City, and the university's flagship innovation hub for Western Visayas. It operates as a component unit of WVSU's Knowledge Technology Transfer & Business Development Center (KTTBDC), alongside the Innovation & Technology Support Office (ITSO) and the Knowledge & Technology Transfer Office (KTTO). BINHI incubates startups across biomedical & healthcare, agri-aqua & green technologies, emerging technologies/ICT, and herbal & natural products. As of early 2025 it had graduated 25 startups, generated 81 jobs, supported 13 active incubatees, and catalyzed ₱3M+ in investments and ₱5M+ in startup revenues.

About the Center

The BINHI TBI Building is a collaborative innovation hub for science, technology, and digital transformation in the region; its expanded facility (inaugurated Sept 16, 2025 with DOST Region VI) now houses four DOST-funded programs — see Housed Programs below. Leadership: Dr. Arnel N. Secondes (KTTBDC Director) and Ted Hyacinth T. Aspera (General Manager), under WVSU's VP for Research, Innovation & Extension.

Programs & Services

  • Startup incubation — In-house and virtual incubation for homegrown startups across BINHI's priority sectors.
  • Business & professional assistance — Mentoring, business modeling, and enterprise development for incubatees.
  • Funding & grants support — Help with proposals and access to DOST and partner funding.
  • Technology forecasting & matching — Linking innovators with technologies, markets, and investors.
  • Facilities access — Laboratory, production, and conference facilities at the BINHI TBI Building.
  • Branding & IP protection — Branding and intellectual-property support via KTTBDC / ITSO.
  • Training & capacity-building — Technopreneurship trainings and innovation programs.

Housed Programs (DOST-funded, launched Sept 16, 2025 in the BINHI TBI Building)

  • AMCen Western Visayas (Advanced Manufacturing Center) — Satellite of the national AMCen under DOST-MIRDC: 3D printing, prototyping, and consultancy.
  • Project SENTINEL — Localized urban-microclimate monitoring via multi-purpose "smart poles" feeding a cloud platform.

Related

  • WVSU High-Performance Computing (HPC) Facility — A separate 2026 initiative under the Western Visayas AI Regional Development Action Plan 2025–2030 (not part of the BINHI building launch).
  • Parent office: KTTBDC (with ITSO and KTTO). Partners: DOST-VI, DILG-VI, DICT-VI, DEPDev-VI, CHED, TESDA, DTI, PCCI.
