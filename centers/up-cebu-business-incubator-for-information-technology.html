UP Cebu Business Incubator for Information Technology (UP Cebu inIT)

UP Cebu · Region VII

InIT UP-Cebu (UP Cebu inIT) is the Incubator for Information Technology incubator hosted by the University of the Philippines Cebu in Cebu City. Operating under UPScale, UP Cebu's umbrella innovation platform, InIT supports pre-revenue and early-stage technology startups with a focus on software, digital services, and tech-driven social enterprises in the Visayas.

About the Center

InIT UP-Cebu is part of the University of the Philippines system's broader commitment to technology commercialization and entrepreneurship, channeling the research and talent of UP Cebu into viable startup ventures that address regional and national challenges.

Programs & Services

  • Tech Startup Incubation — Support for pre-revenue technology startups in software, apps, and digital services
  • Mentoring — Guidance from UP Cebu faculty, UP system alumni, and industry partners
  • Business Development — Business model validation, legal support, and go-to-market strategy
  • Networking — Connections to the UP alumni network, Cebu tech community, and national startup ecosystem
  • Workspace — Co-working and lab facilities within the UP Cebu campus
  • Demo Days — Pitching and networking events with investors and corporate partners
