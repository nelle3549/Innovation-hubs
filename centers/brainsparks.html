Brainsparks is a startup incubator and accelerator based in Metro Manila, founded in 2015 to support Filipino founders driving impactful innovation. It positions itself as an innovation ecosystem booster, helping early-stage entrepreneurs build the foundation, mindset, and principles for sustainable growth.

About the Center

The organization structures its work around three pillars — Innovation Programs, Mentoring and Management Coaching, and Ecosystem Events — and has partnered with local and international organizations to run specialized accelerator initiatives. It remains an active connector within the growing Philippine startup community.

Programs & Services

  • Incubation Program — In-depth, custom-fit guidance to help founders build and grow their companies over an extended engagement.
  • Business Accelerator — Structured acceleration support for startups, including 1:1 business sessions and business coaching.
  • Mentoring & Management Coaching — Specialized coaching and mentorship for founders and startup management teams.
  • Founders Circle — A series of informal meetups connecting founders with industry experts, mentors, investors, and peers.
  • Partner Accelerator Programs — Co-run initiatives with partners, such as the Shell LiveWIRE Accelerator Program and Ecothon Philippines boot camp.
