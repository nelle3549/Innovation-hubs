Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

ISAT-U FabLab

Iloilo Science & Technology University · Region VI

ISAT-U FabLab is a fabrication laboratory hosted by Iloilo Science and Technology University (ISAT-U) in Iloilo City. The FabLab provides students, faculty, researchers, and local entrepreneurs with access to state-of-the-art digital fabrication equipment, enabling hands-on innovation, prototyping, and product development within the ISAT-U campus community.

About the Center

As part of ISAT-U's commitment to applied technology and community service, the FabLab supports the Western Visayas innovation ecosystem by making maker technologies accessible to aspiring engineers, designers, and entrepreneurs across the region.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping services for product models and functional parts
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision fabrication for custom designs, signage, and components
  • CNC Milling/Routing — Computer-controlled machining for engineering and design projects
  • CAD Workstations — Design software access for product and engineering design
  • Vinyl Cutting — Custom stickers, decals, and signage production
  • Student & Faculty Research Support — Fabrication assistance for academic projects, theses, and capstone projects
  • MSME & Community Outreach — Equipment access and training for local entrepreneurs and community members
