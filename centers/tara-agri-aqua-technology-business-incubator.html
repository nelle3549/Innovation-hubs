TARA Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubator (Caraga State University)

Caraga State University (CSU) · Region XIII

CSU TARA (Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubator) Technology Business Incubator is hosted by Caraga State University in Butuan City, Agusan del Norte and serves Region XIII (Caraga). TARA is CSU's dedicated agri-aqua incubation arm — operating alongside the university's Navigatu (iTecH) incubator — and is specifically focused on the Agriculture, Aquatic, and Natural Resources (AANR) sectors relevant to Caraga's resource-rich environment.

About the Center

TARA supports 14 active incubatees developing innovative solutions to enhance food production, minimize post-harvest losses, and promote resource-efficient farming across the region. In June 2026, TARA secured a ₱5 million innovation grant from CHED's Higher Education Development Fund (HEDF), recognizing its role in advancing food and agri-aqua entrepreneurship in the Caraga region.

Together with Navigatu, CSU's TBIs have trained more than 1,500 students and 250 professionals and MSME owners, building a sustainable culture of agri-aqua entrepreneurship in Mindanao. TARA and Navigatu jointly conduct innovation competitions, summits, and bootcamps to identify and accelerate student-led ventures.

Programs & Services

  • SIBOL Incubation Program — A two-year incubation initiative designed to transform promising student ideas into viable technology-based startup ventures through capacity-building, mentoring, business validation, and market linkage support
  • Agri-Aqua Startup Incubation — End-to-end incubation for startups focused on food production improvement, post-harvest loss reduction, aquaculture, and resource-efficient farming technologies in the Caraga region
  • Capacity Building & Training — Workshops and training programs for students, entrepreneurs, and MSME owners on innovation, entrepreneurship, product development, and business management
  • Technology Business Development — Support for incubatees in developing business plans, financial models, market entry strategies, and investor-ready pitches for agri-aqua ventures
  • IP Management & Legal Services — Intellectual property assistance, technology commercialization guidance, and business registration support for early-stage agri-aqua enterprises
  • DOST Funding & Industry Linkages — Connections to DOST grants, CHED programs, and industry partners to scale and sustain incubatee businesses beyond the TBI cycle
