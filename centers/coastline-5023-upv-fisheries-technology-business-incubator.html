COASTLINE 5023 — UPV Fisheries Technology Business Incubator (FTBI)

UP Visayas · Region VI

The DOST-PCAARRD-UPV Fisheries Technology Business Incubator (UPV Fisheries TBI) is a DOST-PCAARRD-funded TBI hosted by UP Visayas in Miagao, Iloilo. As part of the DOST-PCAARRD AANR TBI network, the UPV Fisheries TBI focuses exclusively on incubating fisheries technology ventures — leveraging UP Visayas’s internationally recognized expertise in marine science, fisheries, and aquaculture to support innovators in Western Visayas and beyond.

About the Center

UP Visayas, located on the shores of the Iloilo Strait in Miagao, is the Philippines’ premier university for fisheries and aquaculture science, with distinguished research programs in marine biology, aquaculture technology, coastal management, and fisheries policy. The TBI extends UPV’s research mandate into the entrepreneurship and innovation domain, supporting researchers, graduate students, and industry innovators in commercializing fisheries technologies — particularly solutions relevant to Western Visayas’s rich fishing grounds, seaweed farming, bangus aquaculture, and seafood processing industries.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Structured incubation for fisheries technology ventures, with workspace, mentorship, and business development support from UPV’s fisheries experts
  • Fisheries Technology Innovation — Specialized incubation for startups in aquaculture systems, post-harvest fish processing, marine product development, and seafood value addition
  • Marine Science Applications — Support for ventures applying marine science research to commercial products, including seaweed-derived bioproducts and coastal aquaculture technologies
  • Aquaculture Technology Development — Incubation support for aquaculture innovations including bangus, tilapia, seaweed, and other aquaculture enterprises in Western Visayas
  • Business Development & Market Access — Guidance on enterprise development, seafood market linkage, regulatory compliance, and export readiness for fisheries startups
  • IP Management & Research Commercialization — Support for UPV faculty and researchers converting marine and fisheries research into market-ready technologies and enterprises
  • DOST-PCAARRD Funding Programs — Access to DOST’s AANR funding instruments and national incubation network for fisheries technology scale-up and commercialization
