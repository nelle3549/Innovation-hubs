Food Innovation Center – Batangas State University

Batangas State University (The National Engineering University) · Region IV-A

CALABARZON FIC — Batangas State University is hosted by Batangas State University (The National Engineering University) in Batangas City. It serves as the region's DOST/DTI Food Innovation Center, supporting CALABARZON MSMEs with food product development, processing, and packaging. It is part of the nationwide food innovation center network surfaced in the Startupreneur Innovation Hub.
