Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

HNU FabLab – Center for Innovation

Holy Name University · Region VII

The HNU FabLab – Center for Innovation is a multidisciplinary fabrication and innovation hub hosted by Holy Name University in Tagbilaran City, Bohol (Region VII). Established with support from the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) under the Shared Service Facility (SSF) Program, the center was formally launched on December 16–17, 2025, marking a milestone in advancing innovation, technology, and creative development in the province of Bohol.

About the Center

The FabLab provides students, faculty, entrepreneurs, and micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) with access to digital-fabrication and prototyping technologies, helping turn ideas and digital designs into physical products. It is part of the broader fabrication laboratory network registered on the international FabLabs platform (fablabs.io).

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing of prototypes, parts, and product models.
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision cutting and engraving across various materials.
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Computer-controlled machining for fabrication work.
  • Vinyl Cutting — Production of stickers, labels, and signage.
  • Electronics & PCB Prototyping — Support for designing and testing electronic circuits.
  • Design & Prototyping Support — Assistance converting digital designs into physical prototypes for students, innovators, and MSMEs.
  • Trainings & Facility Access — Capability-building sessions and shared access to advanced fabrication equipment for the Bohol community.
