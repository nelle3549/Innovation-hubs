AdDU ADDVentures Technology Business Incubator (TBI)

Ateneo de Davao University (AdDU) · Region XI

ADDVentures Technology Business Incubator is the innovation incubation center of Ateneo de Davao University (AdDU) in Davao City, serving Region XI (Davao Region). Established through DOST-PCIEERD's HEIRIT (Higher Education Institution Readiness for Innovation and Technopreneurship) Program and formally inaugurated in February 2025, ADDVentures holds the distinction of housing the Philippines' first Aerospace and Robotics-focused Technology Business Incubator.

About the Center

ADDVentures' banner program is ASTRA — Aerospace Technology and Robotics Applications for the Environment and Security — reflecting a unique positioning in the Philippine TBI landscape. While most university-based TBIs focus on food technology, ICT, or agri-aqua innovation, ADDVentures specifically targets startups developing solutions using aerospace technologies and robotics, with applications in disaster resilience, environmental monitoring, and defense — sectors critical to a country as geographically vulnerable as the Philippines.

The center is anchored within Ateneo de Davao's strong engineering, sciences, and social development traditions — combining AdDU's Jesuit values with cutting-edge technology incubation to produce startup ventures that address pressing environmental and security challenges. ADDVentures contributes to Davao City's growing role as a Mindanao innovation hub, working alongside UPGRADE InnoLab, the UM Asenso Hub, the UIC Marian TBI, and IDEAS Davao to build Region XI's startup ecosystem.

Programs & Services

  • Aerospace & Robotics Laboratory Access — Fully equipped aerospace and robotics laboratories enabling incubatees to design, prototype, and test innovations in drone technology, satellite applications, robotic systems, and automation
  • ASTRA Innovation Development — Dedicated R&D and commercialization support for startups developing Aerospace Technology and Robotics Applications for the Environment and Security — including UAV/drone-based solutions, sensor networks, and remote sensing applications
  • Disaster Resilience & Environmental Monitoring Ventures — Specialized mentorship for startups building technology solutions for disaster risk reduction, climate change adaptation, environmental monitoring, and ecosystem management
  • Business Development & Commercialization — Coaching on business model design, market entry, investor pitching, IP protection, and regulatory navigation for deep-tech startups in aerospace and robotics sectors
  • DOST-PCIEERD Funding & Industry Linkages — Access to DOST HEIRIT funding, connections to DOST innovation programs, and facilitation of partnerships with defense, security, and environmental government agencies for qualifying incubatee ventures
