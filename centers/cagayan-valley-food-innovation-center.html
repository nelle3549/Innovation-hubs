Cagayan Valley Food Innovation Center (FIC)

Cagayan State University · Region II

The Cagayan Valley Food Innovation Center (FIC) is hosted by Cagayan State University at its Carig Campus in Tuguegarao City, Cagayan and serves Region II (Cagayan Valley). Established in 2014, it holds the distinction of being the first Food Innovation Center in all of Luzon and the second FIC established in the Philippines — pioneering the DOST FIC model in Northern Luzon after the original FIC launched in Davao City.

About the Center

The center was established to help transform the agriculture-rich Cagayan Valley into a food processing haven — improving and adding value to the region's diverse agri-food resources, from Cagayan's river fisheries and tobacco to its corn, rice, and livestock products. The FIC serves as a one-stop shop for food research and development, enabling local food processors, aspiring entrepreneurs, and researchers to improve existing products, generate new product lines, and access food technology expertise otherwise unavailable to small-scale producers.

The center works in close partnership with DOST-Region II, which coordinates SETUP activities including NUTRIPACK training-workshops that guide MSMEs in science-based food packaging and labeling practices to meet regulatory requirements.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Technical assistance in developing and improving food products from Cagayan Valley's agri-fishery resources, including formulation, shelf-life extension, and food processing technology applications
  • Equipment Access & Shared Processing Facilities — Available equipment includes water retort (glass/PET sterilization), spray dryer, freeze dryer, vacuum fryer, pulverizer/grinder, deep fryer, dough mixer, noodle maker, noodle cutter, and single-screw extruder for pilot-scale production
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Assistance — Guidance on FDA product registration, food safety standards, and GMP compliance for food enterprises in Region II
  • Packaging & Nutrition Labeling — Technical support for nutrition facts calculation, packaging design, and food label compliance aligned with Philippine FDA and DOST standards
  • R&D Support & Technology Transfer — Access to DOST-developed food technologies for adapters and beneficiaries seeking to commercialize proven food processing innovations
