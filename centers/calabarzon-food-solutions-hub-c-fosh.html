Food Innovation Center

CALABARZON Food Solutions Hub — C-FoSH (LSPU FIC)

Laguna State Polytechnic University (LSPU) · Region IV-A

The CALABARZON Food Solutions Hub (C-FoSH) is a DOST-designated Food Innovation Center hosted by Laguna State Polytechnic University (LSPU) at its Sta. Cruz Campus in Sta. Cruz, Laguna. C-FoSH operates as a DTI Shared Service Facility (SSF) managed in partnership with the Association of Laguna Food Processors, Inc. (ALAFOP), LSPU, and DOST-CALABARZON — positioning it as a food processing center that serves the collaborative needs of the CALABARZON food processing industry.

About the Center

C-FoSH provides CALABARZON MSMEs — particularly food processors and agri-food entrepreneurs from Laguna and surrounding provinces — with access to industrial food processing equipment, regulatory guidance, and product development support. As a DTI SSF, C-FoSH helps local MSMEs upgrade existing food products and innovate new ones through shared access to capital-intensive processing equipment. The center is managed by Engr. Mark Augusto V. Agus, Operations and Maintenance Manager, based at LSPU's Sta. Cruz, Laguna campus.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Formulation assistance, recipe optimization, and product development support for new and improved food products from CALABARZON producers
  • Food Processing Services — Contract processing using SSF equipment including vacuum freeze dryer, spray dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer for MSME clients
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Guidance — Assistance with FDA product registration, labeling compliance, and product standards for CALABARZON food enterprises
  • Packaging Design & Nutrition Labeling — Support for packaging development, branding, and accurate nutritional information for commercial food products
  • Product Costing & Market Analysis — Assistance with production cost computation, pricing strategy, and market feasibility assessment for food MSMEs
  • Equipment Rental — Shared access to DTI SSF food processing equipment for CALABARZON MSMEs needing capacity beyond their own facilities
  • MSME Training & Capacity-Building — Seminars, workshops, and technical training for Laguna and CALABARZON food producers in food technology, quality management, and business development
  • Industry Partnership — Collaboration with ALAFOP (Association of Laguna Food Processors, Inc.) for industry-driven food processing support and market development
