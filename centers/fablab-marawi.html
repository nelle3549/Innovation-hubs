Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Marawi

Mindanao State University – Main · BARMM

Fab Lab Marawi is a digital fabrication and arts innovation center established through a collaboration between Mindanao State University – Main (MSU-Marawi) and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), under MSU-Marawi’s Office of the Vice Chancellor for Research, Extension, and Development (OVCRED). Located at Macarambon Hall, 1st Street, MSU Marawi City, the lab was established with a deliberate and distinctive purpose: to serve as a center for reconstruction, economic empowerment, and cultural innovation for Marawi and Lanao del Sur — particularly for MSMEs and communities affected by the 2017 Marawi Siege.

About the Center

Fab Lab Marawi operates simultaneously as a technology access center, training provider, service bureau, and collaboration hub — providing digital fabrication infrastructure that would otherwise be unavailable locally. Its mission uniquely integrates modern technology with Meranaw cultural heritage, capturing and modernizing traditional arts and crafts through digital tools. The center’s tagline: "The center for Meranaw arts preservation and innovation."

Programs & Services

Fabrication equipment and capabilities:

  • 3D Printer Machines — Rapid prototyping, custom parts, and small-batch production
  • Big Milling Machine — Large-scale CNC milling for industrial-grade fabrication
  • Small Milling Machine — Precision milling for smaller components and detail work
  • Laser Cutter Machine — Precision laser cutting for wood, acrylic, and other materials
  • Embroidery Machine — Digital embroidery for textile and fabric innovation, including traditional Meranaw weave integration
  • Print and Cut Machine — Combined printing and cutting for custom packaging and signage
  • Wood Lathe Machine — Wood turning for craft products and custom components
  • CAD Workstations — Computer-aided design support for digital design development

Core service areas:

  • Prototyping and Short-Run Production — Turning MSME product ideas into market-ready prototypes
  • Research and Development — Supporting product R&D and liaising with academic and industry partners
  • Digital Design and Fabrication Training — Training programs for MSMEs, students, and community members
  • Meranaw Arts Innovation — Integrating traditional Meranaw crafts (weaving, woodwork, metalwork) with modern digital fabrication for product development and cultural preservation
  • Machine Utilization Services — Open access to the full equipment suite for registered users

Notable work: Partnership with Maranao Collectibles for 3D-printed Philippine Sports Commission logos supplied to national IP Games; active MSME collaborations across Lanao del Sur.
