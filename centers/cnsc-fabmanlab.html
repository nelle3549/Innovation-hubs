Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

CNSC FABMANLAB (Camarines Norte State College)

Camarines Norte State College (CNSC) · Region V

FabLab Cam Norte is the fabrication laboratory of Camarines Norte State College (CNSC) in Daet, Camarines Norte — now operating as the CNSC Fabrication & Manufacturing Research Center (FMRC). As a member of the global Fab Lab network (listed at fablabs.io/labs/cnscfabmanlab), FabLab Cam Norte provides Camarines Norte with open access to digital fabrication equipment, empowering students, entrepreneurs, MSMEs, and community innovators to design and produce their ideas.

About the Center

Operating within CNSC's research and innovation ecosystem, the FabLab supports the college's engineering, industrial technology, and science programs — giving students hands-on experience with digital manufacturing while providing local MSMEs and inventors access to prototyping capabilities that would otherwise require travel to larger urban centers.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — FDM 3D printing services for product prototypes, engineering models, and educational materials
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision laser cutting and engraving on wood, acrylic, and other materials for product design and small-scale production
  • CNC Milling & Routing — Computer-controlled fabrication for wood and soft materials for architectural models and product components
  • Vinyl Cutting — Digital vinyl cutting for decals, stickers, templates, and signage
  • 3D Scanning — Object digitization for reverse engineering and archival documentation
  • Large-Format Printing — Wide-format printing services for banners, posters, and large-scale visual materials
  • CAD Workstations — Access to computer-aided design software and workstations for product design and engineering drawings
  • MSME & Community Access — Open fabrication sessions for local businesses, students, and Camarines Norte communities seeking affordable prototyping and manufacturing support
