Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Caraga

Caraga State University · Region XIII

FabLab Caraga is the digital fabrication laboratory of Caraga State University, located in Butuan City, Agusan del Norte. As a member of the global Fab Lab network (listed on fablabs.io), the lab provides open access to fabrication equipment for students, faculty, researchers, and MSMEs across the Caraga region. Operating under Caraga State University — the region's flagship state university — the FabLab supports applied research, prototyping, and innovation development for Caraga's communities, many of which are engaged in agriculture, forestry, aquaculture, and resource-based industries.

About the Center

The lab maintains a dedicated Facebook page (facebook.com/fablabcaraga) and operates with an institutional email through CarSU, reflecting its formal integration into the university's research and innovation support infrastructure. The center bridges the gap between academic research and practical application by providing hands-on fabrication tools that transform ideas into physical products and prototypes.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of engineering models, product concepts, agricultural equipment components, and replacement parts
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision processing of acrylic, wood, cardboard, and fabric for design, research, and commercial applications
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Subtractive manufacturing for wood, foam, and soft metals for product and research applications
  • Vinyl Cutting — Custom decals, labels, and signage for product branding and packaging
  • 3D Scanning — Digitizing physical objects for reverse engineering and custom fabrication
  • Large-Format/UV Printing — Wide-format printing for promotional materials, product displays, and packaging
  • CAD Workstations — Access to computer-aided design software for students and entrepreneurs developing product concepts
  • Training & Capacity Building — Workshops on digital fabrication tools, design methodologies, and product development for students, researchers, and MSMEs
