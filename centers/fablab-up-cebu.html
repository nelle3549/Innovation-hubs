Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab UP-Cebu

UP Cebu · Region VII

FabLab UP-Cebu is the digital fabrication laboratory of the University of the Philippines Cebu (UP Cebu), located in Cebu City in the Central Visayas region (Region VII). Listed on the global fablabs.io directory, the lab extends UP's commitment to research, innovation, and public service to Cebu — one of the Philippines' most dynamic economic and creative centers — serving UP Cebu students, faculty, and the broader Cebu innovation ecosystem with professional-grade digital fabrication tools.

About the Center

UP Cebu's position as an autonomous state university in the Visayas gives its FabLab distinctive reach: the lab serves not only engineering and design students but also entrepreneurs, architects, artists, and technology professionals in Cebu's thriving IT-BPM, manufacturing, and creative economy sectors. As a national university FabLab, it represents a high-quality, publicly accessible fabrication resource within the Cebu metropolitan area.

Connected to UP's broader innovation infrastructure and the global Fab Lab network, FabLab UP-Cebu serves as a key node for the Visayas — enabling local innovators to access world-class fabrication capabilities and connect with a worldwide maker community.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing for product prototypes, engineering models, research tools, and artistic works
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision laser processing of wood, acrylic, and materials for design, fabrication, and creative applications
  • CNC Milling/Routing — Computer-controlled machining for precision component fabrication and custom part production
  • Vinyl Cutting — Digital cutting for graphic art, signage, labels, and design applications
  • 3D Scanning — Dimensional scanning for reverse engineering and digital capture of physical objects
  • CAD Workstations — Digital design facilities for technical drawing, engineering design, and product development
  • Open Fabrication Access — Services for UP Cebu students and faculty, and the broader Cebu innovation community through the global Fab Lab network's open-access mission
