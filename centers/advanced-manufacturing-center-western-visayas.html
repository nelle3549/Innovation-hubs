Advanced Manufacturing Center (AMCen) Western Visayas

West Visayas State University (WVSU) · Region VI

The WVSU Advanced Manufacturing Center is a DOST-supported digital fabrication and advanced manufacturing facility at West Visayas State University (WVSU), located in La Paz, Iloilo City in Western Visayas (Region VI). The center extends WVSU's engineering and technology programs into advanced manufacturing, providing digital fabrication tools and technical support to students, faculty researchers, and MSMEs in Iloilo City and the broader Western Visayas region.

About the Center

The center operates in close association with WVSU-BINHI, the university's program for technology-based enterprise development, creating a natural pipeline from fabrication and prototyping to business development and commercialization for Western Visayas innovators and entrepreneurs.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for product prototyping and engineering model fabrication
  • Digital fabrication support for MSME product development and innovation
  • Advanced manufacturing research with WVSU engineering and technology faculty
  • Prototyping services for student innovators and startup teams
  • Training programs in digital manufacturing, CAD/CAM, and advanced fabrication techniques
  • MSME support for enterprises in Western Visayas seeking to upgrade production and product design capabilities
