CSPC ASOG Technology Business Incubator

Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges (CSPC) · Region V

The CSPC ASOG Technology Business Incubator is hosted by Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges (CSPC) in Nabua, Camarines Sur and serves the Bicol Region (Region V). Funded by DOST-PCIEERD under the HEIRIT (Higher Education Institution Readiness for Innovation and Technopreneurship) Program, ASOG TBI formally launched its Incubation Center on March 23, 2026 at the CSPC Auditorium — marking a milestone in CSPC's commitment to advancing innovation and entrepreneurship in Bicol.

About the Center

ASOG TBI's banner program is "Engineering and AI-Based Solutions for Food Value Chain Management" (operating under the ALTITUDE Incubation Program), positioning it as Bicol's dedicated hub for engineering and artificial intelligence applications in the food sector. The center serves as a dynamic platform where startups and MSMEs in food technology and food value-chain management can access DOST-backed technical support, mentorship, and business development services through its official hub at asogtbi.com.

The ASOG TBI aims to support CSPC's mission to develop globally competitive polytechnic graduates and entrepreneurs — leveraging the college's engineering programs and AI capabilities to drive food system innovation across the Bicol Region.

Programs & Services

  • Engineering & AI-Based Food Tech Incubation — ALTITUDE Program incubation support for startups developing engineering and AI-powered solutions in food production, food safety, processing, logistics, and value chain management
  • Technology Business Mentorship — Expert coaching from faculty engineers, industry mentors, and DOST-PCIEERD advisors on product development, technical validation, and scale-up strategy for food tech ventures
  • Startup Development & Business Planning — End-to-end business development support for early-stage startups including business model design, market analysis, pitch preparation, and regulatory guidance
  • Innovation Events & Ecosystem Building — Hackathons, demo days, startup competitions, and innovation forums to foster a culture of technopreneurship and identify promising ventures at CSPC and across Bicol
  • Incubation Space & Technical Facilities — Access to co-working space, engineering laboratories, and shared facilities at CSPC for incubatees developing and testing food technology products
  • DOST Funding & Industry Linkages — Connections to DOST-PCIEERD startup grants, food industry networks, and investor contacts to help incubatees access capital and scale their businesses
