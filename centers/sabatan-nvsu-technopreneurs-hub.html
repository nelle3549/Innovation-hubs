Sabatan — NVSU Technopreneurs Hub

Nueva Vizcaya State University · Region II

Sabatan — NVSU Technopreneurs Hub is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Nueva Vizcaya State University (NVSU) in Bayombong, Nueva Vizcaya. Under DOST's banner program for Metal Technologies, Sabatan supports student entrepreneurs, researchers, and MSMEs in developing innovations across metalworking, tooling, fabrication, and related manufacturing processes.

About the Center

The "Sabatan" name reflects a local cultural identity, rooting the TBI's mission in the community it serves. As part of the DOST-TAPI national TBI network, Sabatan provides incubatees with structured support from concept to commercialization, drawing on NVSU's expertise in engineering and applied technology.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Workspace, mentoring, and operational support for metal technology ventures
  • Technology Commercialization — Assistance moving engineering research and innovations to market
  • DOST Funding Access — Linkage to DOST grants, TECHNICOM, and innovation funding programs
  • Business Development — Business planning, IP management, and legal structuring support
  • Mentoring — Guidance from NVSU faculty, industry experts, and DOST-TAPI mentors
  • Community Engagement — Programs supporting local MSMEs in metalworking and fabrication
  • Demo Days & Showcases — Opportunities to pitch to investors and showcase innovations
