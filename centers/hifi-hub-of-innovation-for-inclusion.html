HiFi — Hub of Innovation for Inclusion

De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde · NCR

HiFi — Hub of Innovation for Inclusion is a DOST-accredited Technology Business Incubator hosted by De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde in Malate, Manila (NCR). Founded in 2016 and housed at the Peter D. Garrucho Innovations Institute, HiFi is dedicated to driving social innovation and inclusion, nurturing financially viable startups into sustainable enterprises.

About the Center

HiFi launched as a DOST-accredited TBI in March 2023 in partnership with the DOST-Philippine Council for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD), ranking first among 27 universities awarded TBI grants. Reflecting Benilde's creative heritage, the incubator focuses on startups in the digital, interactive, and design domains, as well as ventures advancing inclusion and social impact.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — End-to-end incubation nurturing startups from early stage to sustainable operations.
  • Pre-Incubation & Startup Curation — Selection and early mentoring of ventures in the digital, interactive, and design domains.
  • Workshops & Technopreneurship Training — Skill-building sessions, talks, and activities fostering innovation and inclusion.
  • Mentoring & Advisory — Guidance from mentors and industry experts across the incubation journey.
  • Crowdfunding & Fundraising Support — Assistance in raising capital, including community crowdfunding for social ventures.
  • Acceleration Services — Support to help maturing startups scale their operations.
  • DOST-PCIEERD Funding Linkage — Assistance in accessing DOST grants and startup funding opportunities.
