Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Santiago City

LGU-Santiago City · Region II

FabLab Santiago City is a digital fabrication center operated by the Local Government Unit of Santiago City, located in Santiago City, Isabela in the Cagayan Valley region. Listed on the global Fab Lab network directory (fablabs.io), the lab provides open access to fabrication equipment and digital design tools for entrepreneurs, students, artisans, and community members in Santiago City and the surrounding Cagayan Valley area.

About the Center

As an LGU-operated Fab Lab, the center is part of Santiago City's commitment to community-driven innovation and economic development. Santiago City, an independent component city and regional growth center in Isabela, uses the FabLab to bridge the gap between local talent and the tools needed to develop competitive, technology-based products — particularly in crafts, agri-processing, and light manufacturing aligned with the region's economic profile.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of product models, custom components, and replacement parts for local enterprises
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision processing of acrylic, wood, cardboard, and leather for crafts, product packaging, and signage
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Subtractive manufacturing for wood, foam, and soft metals suited to furniture, crafts, and light manufacturing
  • Vinyl Cutting — Custom decals, labels, and stickers for branding and product packaging
  • Electronics & Embedded Systems — Support for Arduino/microcontroller-based product prototypes and automation projects
  • CAD Workstations — Access to computer-aided design software for product and prototype development
  • Community Innovation Programs — Open workshops and training sessions on digital fabrication tools, product design, and entrepreneurship for Santiago City residents
  • MSME Support — End-to-end assistance for local businesses developing or improving products using digital fabrication technologies
