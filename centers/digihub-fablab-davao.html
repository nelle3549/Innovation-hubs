Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

DIGIHUB FabLab Davao

University of Southeastern Philippines (USeP) · Region XI

DIGIHUB FabLab Davao is the digital fabrication laboratory of the University of Southeastern Philippines (USeP), located in Davao City. Listed on the global Fab Lab network directory (fablabs.io), DIGIHUB provides open access to fabrication equipment and digital design tools for students, faculty, researchers, and MSMEs in the Davao Region (Region XI). The center’s name — DIGIHUB — reflects its role as a digital innovation hub, combining fabrication capabilities with creative and technical support for the Davao startup and maker community.

About the Center

USeP is a leading state university in Southern Mindanao, with strong programs in engineering, technology, and applied sciences. DIGIHUB FabLab extends the university’s innovation mandate by providing hands-on prototyping tools that support product development, student research, and technology entrepreneurship. The center serves as a gateway for Davao Region innovators to rapidly prototype and develop their ideas into tangible products and enterprises.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of engineering models, product designs, functional parts, and research specimens for students, researchers, and MSMEs
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision processing of acrylic, wood, cardboard, leather, and other materials for product, craft, and research applications
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Subtractive manufacturing for wood, foam, and soft metals for product prototyping and design applications
  • Vinyl Cutting — Custom decals, signage, labels, and stickers for product branding and packaging
  • Electronics & Embedded Systems — Support for Arduino/microcontroller-based product prototypes and IoT device development
  • Large-Format/UV Printing — Wide-format printing for signage, banners, research posters, and product displays
  • CAD Workstations — Access to computer-aided design software for students and innovators developing digital product designs
  • Training & Workshops — Capacity-building sessions on digital fabrication tools, design methodologies, and maker skills for students and community innovators
