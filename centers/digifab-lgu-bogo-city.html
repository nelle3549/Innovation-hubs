About the Center

DigiFab – LGU Bogo City is a fabrication laboratory established by the Local Government Unit (LGU) of Bogo City in Cebu. As a community-oriented FabLab, DigiFab provides residents, students, and local entrepreneurs in Bogo City access to digital fabrication equipment and maker technologies, supporting grassroots innovation and MSME development in northern Cebu.

The center is part of the growing network of government-supported FabLabs across the Philippines, bringing prototyping and digital manufacturing capabilities closer to local communities outside Metro Cebu.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping services for product development and modeling
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision cutting and engraving for signage, parts, and custom products
  • Digital Fabrication Training — Workshops on CAD software and digital fabrication tools
  • MSME & Community Support — Accessible services for local entrepreneurs, students, and community members
  • Innovation Support — Assistance for product prototyping and local enterprise development
