About the Center

WPU-ATBI is the DOST-PCAARRD–funded Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubator, described on its own page as "a project funded by DOST-PCAARRD which aims to provide services towards commercialization." It is the older and larger presence — 1.8K followers — and carries the incubatee spotlights and commercialization programming.

HATCH — Holistic Agri-Aqua Technology Commercialization Hub — is the newer brand, tagline "Empowering Farmers, Transforming Aquaculture." Its page (472 followers) fronts the same office and the same staff, and its posts credit ACEF alongside the HATCH mark, indicating a distinct funding line from the PCAARRD project.

🔗 One centre, two brands. Both pages resolve to the same office and share the single published email aatbi.dahatch@wpu.edu.ph. They are maintained as separate identities publicly, so both are documented here rather than split into two records. The da in the shared address points to a Department of Agriculture line for HATCH, sitting alongside DOST-PCAARRD's funding of WPU-ATBI.

Programs & Services

  • Technology commercialization support — WPU-ATBI's stated purpose is to move agri-aqua research toward commercialization, bridging WPU's R&D output and the market.
  • Incubatee development — the incubator profiles and supports community enterprises; the WPU-ATBI Incubatee Spotlight series features groups such as the San Jose Aquaculture SLP Association.
  • Aquaculture enterprise assistance — HATCH-branded work concentrates on fisherfolk and aquaculture producers, including the Aporawan Patis Processors and Fisherfolk Association.
  • Farmer and fisherfolk enablement — technical and business support to primary producers in Palawan, consistent with HATCH's "Empowering Farmers, Transforming Aquaculture" positioning.
  • Facilities — the WPU-ATBI Office is housed at the ABBA R&D Center on the WPU Puerto Princesa Campus, open to walk-in engagement.
  • How to engage — email aatbi.dahatch@wpu.edu.ph, or contact either brand's page directly. WPU-ATBI is the more actively followed channel.
