Technology Business Incubator

SIKAT TBI

Bataan Peninsula State University · Region III

SIKAT TBI is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Bataan Peninsula State University (BPSU) in Balanga City, Bataan, Region III (Central Luzon). Launched by the Department of Science and Technology, SIKAT (Synergizing Innovation and Creativity through Cultural Knowledge, Artistry, and Technology) is designed to bring together academia, government, and industry to strengthen the country's startup ecosystem through science, technology, and innovation. With its establishment, BPSU became one of the higher education institutions assisted by DOST to operate its own TBI.

About the Center

Funded by the DOST-Philippine Council for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD) under the HEIRIT program, SIKAT TBI provides the resources, services, and facilities that technology-based startups need during their development stage. Its goal is to nurture firms that leave the incubation program financially viable and able to sustain operations and compete in the market.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — Structured support developing technology-based startups into financially viable enterprises.
  • Pre-Incubation & Ideation — Early-stage guidance for faculty, students, and community innovators shaping business ideas.
  • Mentoring & Advisory — Coaching from mentors, industry partners, and technical experts.
  • Technopreneurship Training — Capacity-building on innovation, business modeling, and enterprise development.
  • Co-Working Space & Facilities — Workspace and resources for incubatee startups.
  • Academe-Industry-Government Linkage — Networking connecting startups with industry and government partners.
  • DOST-PCIEERD Grant & Funding Linkage — Assistance in accessing DOST seed funding and startup grants.
  • Market Linkage & Commercialization Support — Connections to markets and guidance on bringing products to market.
