Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Pagadian — JHCSC

Josefina H. Cerilles State College · Region IX

FabLab Pagadian is the digital fabrication laboratory of Josefina H. Cerilles State College (JHCSC), located in Pagadian City in the Zamboanga Peninsula (Region IX). As the primary higher education institution in Zamboanga del Sur and a key state university in western Mindanao, JHCSC operates the FabLab as a resource for student innovation and MSME support in the manufacturing and technology sector of the Peninsula.

About the Center

The center provides digital fabrication services to students, researchers, and local entrepreneurs in Pagadian City and the broader Zamboanga Peninsula — extending modern fabrication technologies to a region where such resources have historically been limited, and enabling local innovators to prototype and produce physical products at accessible cost.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for product prototyping and model fabrication
  • Laser cutting and engraving for materials customization and crafts
  • CNC milling/routing for wood, acrylic, and component fabrication
  • Vinyl cutting for signage, graphics, and labeling
  • CAD workstations for design and pre-production planning
  • Fabrication support for MSMEs and student projects in the Zamboanga Peninsula
  • Open-access fabrication services for the local technology and entrepreneurship community
