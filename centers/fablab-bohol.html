Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab Bohol

Bohol Island State University · Region VII

FabLab Bohol is the digital fabrication laboratory of Bohol Island State University (BISU), located in Tagbilaran City, Bohol. Listed on the global Fab Lab network directory (fablabs.io), the lab provides open access to fabrication equipment and digital design tools for students, faculty, community members, and local MSMEs across Bohol Island.

About the Center

Bohol Island State University is the leading state university of Bohol, with campuses across the island serving the province’s educational and research needs. FabLab Bohol maintains a dedicated Facebook page (facebook.com/fablabbohol) and is integrated into BISU’s research and innovation support infrastructure. The center bridges the gap between academic learning and practical innovation, providing Bohol’s students and entrepreneurs with the tools to develop technology-based products relevant to the island’s economy — including tourism, crafts, agribusiness, and food processing.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of models, product designs, engineering parts, and custom components for students, researchers, and MSMEs
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision processing of acrylic, wood, cardboard, and leather for product, craft, and souvenir applications particularly relevant to Bohol’s tourism sector
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Subtractive manufacturing for wood, foam, and soft metals for product prototyping and design
  • Vinyl Cutting — Custom decals, labels, and stickers for product branding, packaging, and signage
  • Electronics & Embedded Systems — Support for microcontroller-based product prototypes, IoT devices, and electronics projects
  • 3D Scanning — Digitizing physical objects for reverse engineering, cultural heritage documentation, and custom fabrication
  • Large-Format/UV Printing — Wide-format printing for promotional materials, product displays, and event signage
  • Training & Community Innovation — Workshops and training sessions on digital fabrication tools, design, and product development for BISU students and Bohol community members
