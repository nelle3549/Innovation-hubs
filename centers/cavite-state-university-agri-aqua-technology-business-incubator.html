Cavite State University Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubator (CvSU-ATBI)

Cavite State University · Region IV-A

The DOST-PCAARRD-CvSU Agriculture & Food TBI is hosted by Cavite State University in Indang, Cavite and serves Region IV-A (CALABARZON). Implemented through CvSU's CEMDS and CAFENR units, the center is part of the DOST-PCAARRD national Agri-Aqua Technology Business Incubation (ATBI) network under the RAISE IV programme.

About the Center

CvSU ATBI received ₱30.1 million from PCAARRD for its second phase of implementation, significantly expanding its capacity to serve agricultural entrepreneurs in the province. The center focuses on crop production, food processing, and fishery enterprises — key sectors in Cavite's diverse agricultural economy — equipping incubatees with the skills and resources to develop commercially viable technology-based businesses.

The center participates in the RAISE IV InnoVenture program and has served as a benchmarking destination for other TBIs nationwide, reflecting its recognized quality of operations. CvSU ATBI also engages in the DOST-PCAARRD SciCAT (Science for Change Acceleration Technology) initiative, distributing and commercializing university-developed agri-technologies in its province.

Programs & Services

  • Agri-Food Technology Business Incubation — Full incubation cycle support for crop production, food processing, and fishery enterprises using CvSU-developed and DOST-PCAARRD technologies
  • Business Model & Pitching Development — Structured training on business model canvas development, branding, promotional materials creation, and investor pitch preparation to prepare incubatees for markets and financing
  • Scaling & Enterprise Management — Guidance on enterprise scaling strategies, operations management, supply chain development, and market expansion for growing agri-food businesses
  • RAISE IV InnoVenture Program — Participation in PCAARRD's Regional Agri-Aqua Innovation System Enhancement Phase IV, connecting CvSU incubatees to regional innovation networks and co-incubation opportunities
  • SciCAT Technology Distribution — Facilitation of science-based agri-technology adoption and commercialization under the DOST-PCAARRD Science for Change Acceleration Technology program in Cavite
  • Business Development Advisory — Expert coaching on financial planning, market research, packaging design, and distribution strategy throughout the incubation journey
  • DOST Funding & IP Support — Access to PCAARRD grants, DOST startup programs, intellectual property protection assistance, and regulatory compliance guidance
