About the Center

The FARM-A Lab (Fabrication & Automation Research for the Mechanization of Agriculture) at Mariano Marcos State University (MMSU) in Batac City, Ilocos Norte is a specialized digital fabrication and applied research laboratory dedicated to engineering solutions for agricultural mechanization and automation. As one of MMSU's applied research units, the FARM-A Lab serves the smallholder farmers of Region I's predominantly agricultural economy — addressing the persistent need for affordable, locally adapted farm machinery and automation tools suited to the Ilocos landscape.

The lab operates at the intersection of fabrication technology and agricultural engineering, using 3D printing, CNC machining, and automation R&D to prototype, test, and refine new farm implements and post-harvest equipment. MMSU, as the state university of Ilocos Norte, is strategically positioned to lead agricultural mechanization R&D for Region I's garlic, onion, and vegetable farming communities — sectors that stand to benefit significantly from improved mechanization and reduced manual labor intensity.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping — Additive manufacturing services for farm tool components, machine parts, and agricultural equipment prototypes, enabling fast iteration in the design and refinement of mechanized farming solutions
  • CNC Milling & Fabrication — Computer-controlled machining for precision fabrication of agricultural machinery parts, implements, and equipment components for regional farmers and MMSU research projects
  • Agricultural Automation R&D — Applied research on automation systems, sensor-based tools, and mechanized processes for crop planting, harvesting, sorting, and post-harvest handling adapted to the Ilocos farming context
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Laser processing services for custom component fabrication and production of precision parts for agricultural equipment prototypes and innovation projects
  • Student Innovation Projects — Incubation of MMSU engineering student projects involving agricultural machinery, automation design, and farm technology through hands-on access to the lab's fabrication equipment and faculty expertise
  • Technology Extension for Farmers — Demonstration and dissemination of fabricated agricultural tools and mechanization prototypes to farmer cooperatives, agri-businesses, and local government units across Region I
