Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab CTU-Tuburan

Cebu Technological University – Tuburan · Region VII

FabLab CTU-Tuburan is the digital fabrication laboratory of Cebu Technological University – Tuburan, located in the municipality of Tuburan in the western part of Cebu Island. Listed on the global Fab Lab network directory (fablabs.io), the lab provides open access to prototyping and fabrication equipment for students, faculty, community members, and local MSMEs in western Cebu.

About the Center

CTU-Tuburan, as a campus of Cebu Technological University, serves the educational and developmental needs of the western Cebu community. The FabLab extends this mission by providing hands-on fabrication tools that support entrepreneurship, product innovation, and technology-based learning among students and local communities — many of whom are engaged in agriculture, fishing, and cottage industries.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of models, product concepts, and custom components
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision processing of acrylic, wood, cardboard, and similar materials for product and craft applications
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Subtractive manufacturing for wood, foam, and light metals
  • Vinyl Cutting — Custom decals, signage, and labeling for product packaging and branding
  • Electronics & Embedded Systems — Support for Arduino-based product prototypes and IoT device development
  • Large-Format/UV Printing — Wide-format printing for signage, banners, and product display materials
  • CAD Workstations — Computer-aided design software access for students and community members developing product designs
  • Training & Workshops — Capacity-building sessions on fabrication tools and digital design for students and community innovators
