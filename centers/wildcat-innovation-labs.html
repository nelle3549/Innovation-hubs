WildCat Innovation Labs (CIT-U TBI)

Cebu Institute of Technology – University · Region VII

WildCat Innovation Labs is the Technology Business Incubator of Cebu Institute of Technology – University (CIT-U) in Cebu City, supported by DOST-PCIEERD. Named after the university's wildcat mascot, WildCat Innovation Labs serves as CIT-U's dedicated startup incubation and innovation center — supporting technology-based ventures in the Visayas, with a particular thematic focus on AI/ML, IoT, robotics, data analytics, and mechatronics.

About the Center

CIT-U is one of the Philippines' leading technology universities, renowned for its engineering, computing, and technology programs in the Visayas region. WildCat Innovation Labs extends CIT-U's technology education mandate into entrepreneurship and innovation, connecting student innovators, faculty researchers, and tech entrepreneurs with structured incubation support, mentorship, and access to the national startup ecosystem. The lab's dedication to AI/ML and IoT aligns with CIT-U's strong computing and engineering disciplines, while its food innovation focus reflects the appetite for agri-food tech opportunities in Region VII.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Structured incubation program for technology-based ventures, with workspace, mentorship, and business development support for early-stage CIT-U startups
  • AI/ML & IoT Development — Specialized mentorship and technical support for startups developing artificial intelligence, machine learning, and Internet of Things applications
  • Business Development Advisory — Guidance on business planning, market validation, product-market fit, and investor readiness for technology startups
  • IP Protection & Legal Advisory — Assistance with intellectual property filing, patent applications, and legal compliance for innovation enterprises
  • DOST-PCIEERD Program Access — Connections to DOST funding instruments, Startup PH ecosystem, and the national TBI network for scale-up and commercialization support
  • Demo Days & Innovation Events — Pitch competitions, demo days, and startup events connecting WildCat incubatees with investors and industry partners
