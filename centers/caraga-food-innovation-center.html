Caraga Food Innovation Center (FIC)

Caraga State University (CSU) · Region XIII

The Caraga Food Innovation Center (FIC) is hosted by Caraga State University (CSU) in Butuan City, Agusan del Norte and is the lead facility of the Caraga Food Innovation Consortium (CFIC) — a collaborative regional network coordinated by DOST-Caraga that brings together multiple state universities across Region XIII. The CFIC partners include Agusan del Sur State College of Agriculture and Technology (ASSCAT), North Eastern Mindanao State University (NEMSU), and Surigao del Norte State University (SNSU), working together to establish satellite Food Innovation Centers across Caraga's provinces.

About the Center

The center is committed to leading food industry innovation in Caraga — serving as a one-stop hub for MSMEs, local food processors, and agricultural producers seeking to improve existing products, develop new value-added food items, and leverage underutilized crops and resources available across the region's rich natural environment.

In April 2025, DOST-Caraga further strengthened Butuan City's food innovation ecosystem with the launch of the Caraga Food Testing & Innovation Hub at the DOST Caraga Regional Office — complementing the CSU FIC with accredited food safety testing and quality assurance services, and reinforcing Caraga's position as Region XIII's center for agri-food R&D and food enterprise development.

Programs & Services

  • Food Product Development — Technical assistance in developing new food products using locally available Caraga agricultural and fishery resources, including value-adding of fresh produce through processing, encapsulation, and other DOST-developed technologies
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Assistance — Guidance on FDA product registration, food safety standards, and GMP compliance to help MSMEs meet national food safety requirements
  • Packaging & Nutrition Labeling — Assistance with packaging design, nutrition facts labeling, and compliance with FDA and DTI food labeling requirements to make products market-ready
  • Product Costing & Marketing Strategy — Support in computing production costs, pricing strategies, market positioning, and go-to-market planning for food product launches
  • Equipment Rental & Shared Facilities — Access to DOST-funded processing equipment including spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer for pilot-scale production
  • MSME Training & Capacity Building — Training programs for food entrepreneurs on food processing technologies, food safety protocols, and good manufacturing practices (GMP)
