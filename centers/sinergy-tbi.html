SINERGY TBI (Silliman Innovations on Energy)

Silliman University · Region XVIII: NIR

SINERGY TBI (Silliman Innovations on Energy) is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Silliman University in Dumaguete, Negros Oriental. Operating under DOST's banner program for Energy Technology, SINERGY TBI supports entrepreneurs, researchers, and student innovators developing solutions in renewable energy, energy efficiency, and sustainable power systems.

About the Center

As part of the DOST-PCIEERD national TBI network, SINERGY TBI connects incubatees with funding pathways, industry mentors, and technology transfer resources from one of the Philippines' oldest and most respected private universities in the Visayas region.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Workspace, mentoring, and business development support for energy technology ventures
  • Technology Commercialization — Assistance in moving research outputs from laboratory to market
  • Business Development — Support for business planning, legal structuring, and IP management
  • DOST Funding Access — Linkage to DOST-PCIEERD grants, TECHNICOM, and other national funding programs
  • Mentoring & Networking — Access to industry experts, Silliman University alumni, and the broader DOST TBI network
  • Demo Days & Pitching — Opportunities to present ventures to investors and potential partners
