DOST-PCAARRD-BSU Agriculture & Food TBI

Benguet State University · CAR

The DOST-PCAARRD-BSU Agriculture & Food TBI (now also operating as an Innovation Center, ATBI/IC) is hosted by Benguet State University in La Trinidad, Benguet and serves the Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR). Established under the DOST-PCAARRD ATBI Program, BSU ATBI/IC provides a stimulating physical environment and professional support services for start-up and early-stage micro-scale agricultural and food enterprises.

About the Center

As CAR's dedicated agri-based incubator, BSU ATBI/IC bridges smallholder farmers, food processors, and market intermediaries — equipping them with technical know-how, business skills, and linkages to transform agricultural innovations into commercially viable ventures. The center aims to nurture 50 start-up companies per incubation cycle (5–7 years), targeting improvements in income and living standards across the Cordillera highlands.

BSU ATBI/IC works with universities, research institutions, and industry partners to generate employment and entrepreneurship in the highland agricultural economy, with strong focus on the Cordillera's signature crops — vegetables, cut flowers, coffee, and highland-grown processed food products.

Programs & Services

  • Smallholder Farming Incubation — Hands-on training and support for highland smallholder farmers to adopt improved crop production technologies and scale their agri-based enterprises
  • Food Processing Incubation — Access to processing facilities, product development guidance, and regulatory compliance support for food MSMEs entering new markets
  • Marketing Incubation — Business coaching, brand development, and market linkage support to help incubatees grow sales locally and nationally
  • Technical Assistance — Expert guidance on crop production, post-harvest handling, and food technology from BSU researchers and DOST specialists
  • Business Development Services — Business planning, financial modeling, and product commercialization support throughout the structured incubation journey
  • IP & Legal Guidance — Assistance in registering intellectual property, navigating FDA and DTI/SEC registration requirements, and technology transfer documentation
  • Funding & Partnership Linkages — Connections to DOST grants, PCAARRD programs, and private sector investors to sustain and scale incubatee businesses
