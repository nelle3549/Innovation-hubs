Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

CIT-U AI FabLab

Cebu Institute of Technology-University · Region VII

The CIT-U AI FabLab is an Artificial Intelligence Fabrication Laboratory hosted by Cebu Institute of Technology-University (CIT-U) in Cebu City (Region VII). Launched on May 8, 2025, in partnership with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) under the Shared Service Facility (SSF) Program, it is the first AI-integrated fabrication laboratory in Central Visayas and the eighth FabLab in Cebu. The facility was established through a government investment of approximately PhP4.98 million.

About the Center

The AI FabLab empowers students, entrepreneurs, and micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) with access to advanced digital prototyping tools and AI-powered solutions that support education, enhance business operations, and accelerate industry innovation. Among its projects is S.W.A.N. (Smart Waterborne Autonomous Navigator), an aquatic drone designed to monitor water quality in rivers, lakes, and coastal areas.

Programs & Services

  • AI Training & Consultation — AI-powered training and design consultation for students, entrepreneurs, and MSMEs.
  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing of prototypes, parts, and product models.
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — CO2 laser cutting plus fiber laser metal engraving and cutting.
  • CNC Milling — Desktop CNC milling machine for precision fabrication.
  • UV & Large-Format Printing — UV printing, large-format printing, and T-shirt printing services.
  • Robotics & IoT Prototyping — Robotic arm with AI and IoT capabilities for advanced prototyping.
  • Design & Prototyping Support — Assistance turning digital designs into physical prototypes for the Cebu innovation community.
