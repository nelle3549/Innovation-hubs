Food Innovation Center

Eastern Visayas FIC (EV-FIC) — Eastern Visayas State University

Eastern Visayas State University · Region VIII

The Eastern Visayas Food Innovation Center (EV-FIC) is a joint initiative of the Department of Science and Technology and Eastern Visayas State University (EVSU), established at EVSU's campus in Tacloban City, Leyte. Launched on September 26, 2015, it operates as Region VIII's designated one-stop-shop for food processing innovation — serving MSMEs, food entrepreneurs, and researchers across Eastern Visayas with product development, technical training, and laboratory services.

About the Center

The EV-FIC is part of DOST's nationwide Food Innovation Center network, which brings proven DOST-developed food processing technologies to local enterprises across the Philippines. Through its partnership with EVSU's research and development infrastructure, the center bridges academic expertise and industry capacity to help Eastern Visayas food processors produce higher-quality, market-competitive products.

Programs & Services

  • Product & Process Development — R&D assistance for developing new food products, improving existing formulations, and scaling production processes for market readiness
  • Food Safety & Regulatory Compliance — Technical guidance on food safety standards, FDA labeling requirements, and certification pathways for MSMEs
  • Packaging & Nutrition Labeling — Design assistance for product packaging and mandatory nutritional information compliance
  • Product Costing & Market Strategy — Business development support for pricing, costing, and market positioning of food products
  • Laboratory & Testing Services — Chemical, microbiological, and sensory evaluation services coordinated through the DOST regional network
  • Food Processing Equipment Access — DOST-standard equipment including spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer for prototype and small-batch production
  • MSME Training Programs — Workshops on food technology, processing techniques, food entrepreneurship, and industry-standard production practices
  • Technology Transfer — Access to DOST-ITDI and DOST-PCAARRD–developed food processing technologies for commercialization by regional food enterprises
