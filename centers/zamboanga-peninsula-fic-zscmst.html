Zamboanga Peninsula FIC — ZSCMST

Zamboanga State College of Marine Sciences & Technology (ZSCMST) · Region IX

The Zamboanga Peninsula Food Innovation Center (ZamPen FIC) is a DOST-supported Food Innovation Center (FIC) hosted by Zamboanga State College of Marine Sciences and Technology (ZSCMST), located in Zamboanga City, Region IX. As the peninsula's primary FIC, ZamPen FIC serves MSME food producers across the Zamboanga Peninsula — a region whose coastal identity gives the center a distinctive focus on marine-based food products.

About the Center

ZSCMST's mandate in marine sciences and fisheries technology provides ZamPen FIC with specialized expertise in seafood processing and marine food product development — complementing the standard DOST FIC machinery suite (spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, vacuum fryer) with domain knowledge in the region's fishery and aquaculture sectors.

Programs & Services

  • Food product development for marine-based and aquaculture products: fish, shellfish, seaweed, and seafood value-added items
  • Food processing using spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer
  • Food safety and regulatory compliance assistance (FDA registration, product labeling)
  • Packaging design and nutrition labeling support
  • Product costing and market analysis
  • Food product testing and quality evaluation
  • Equipment rental and processing facility access for MSMEs
  • MSME training in seafood processing, food safety, and value chain development
