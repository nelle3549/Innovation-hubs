Technology Business Incubator

ANLAG TA BUKSU-TBI

Bukidnon State University · Region X

ANLAG TA BUKSU-TBI is a Technology Business Incubator hosted by Bukidnon State University (BUKSU) in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon, Region X (Northern Mindanao). As one of the few university-based technology business incubators in Mindanao, it is designed to foster technology-driven entrepreneurship and innovation across the region, providing critical infrastructure, resources, and business support to new ventures.

About the Center

The incubator leverages the region's strengths through its HEART focus — Horticulture, Eco-tourism, Agriculture, Research, and Technology — nurturing technology-based startups particularly in agriculture and eco-tourism. It provides a platform for faculty, students, researchers, and alumni to engage in entrepreneurial activities and supports the commercialization of university-developed technologies into market-ready solutions.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — Structured support for technology-based startups from ideation to market entry.
  • Pre-Incubation & Ideation — Early-stage guidance for faculty, students, researchers, and alumni developing business concepts.
  • Office & Laboratory Space — Access to co-working space and specialized equipment for prototyping and product testing.
  • Mentoring & Coaching — Guidance from seasoned entrepreneurs and industry experts.
  • Technopreneurship Training — Capacity-building on innovation, business modeling, and enterprise development.
  • Funding & Investor Linkage — Connections to investors, funding agencies, and government support to accelerate growth.
  • Technology Transfer & Commercialization — Assistance in transferring university-developed technologies to the market.
  • DOST-PCIEERD Grant Linkage — Support in accessing DOST seed funding and startup grant opportunities.
