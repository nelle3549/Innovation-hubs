Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

Zampen FabLab — ZCSPC

Zamboanga City State Polytechnic College · Region IX

Zampen FabLab — short for Zamboanga Peninsula FabLab — is the digital fabrication laboratory of Zamboanga City State Polytechnic College (ZCSPC), located in Zamboanga City in the Zamboanga Peninsula (Region IX). Listed on the global fablabs.io directory as the representative fabrication laboratory of the Zamboanga Peninsula, Zampen FabLab provides advanced digital manufacturing tools to students, engineering faculty, and local entrepreneurs in Zamboanga City and the surrounding peninsula.

About the Center

ZCSPC's polytechnic character — with its strong engineering and technology programs — gives Zampen FabLab a practical orientation, emphasizing applied fabrication skills and hands-on maker education in a region where modern prototyping infrastructure has historically been underrepresented.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for product prototyping and engineering model fabrication
  • Laser cutting and engraving for precision material processing and crafts
  • CNC milling/routing for component and part manufacture
  • Vinyl cutting for signage, labels, and graphic applications
  • CAD workstations for digital design and technical drawing
  • Open fabrication access for ZCSPC students and faculty
  • Fabrication support for MSMEs and innovators across the Zamboanga Peninsula
