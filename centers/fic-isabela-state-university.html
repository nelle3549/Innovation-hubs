Food Innovation Center

FIC — Isabela State University (Cauayan Campus)

Isabela State University · Region II

The Isabela State University Food Innovation Center (ISU FIC) is a DOST-supported food technology facility at Isabela State University, located at the Cauayan Campus in Cauayan City, Isabela. The center serves as Region II's key food innovation resource, supporting food MSMEs and agri-entrepreneurs in Isabela and the broader Cagayan Valley region.

About the Center

ISU's strong agricultural orientation — the Cagayan Valley is one of the Philippines' most productive agricultural regions — gives the FIC a natural emphasis on value-adding agricultural products, from Isabela's corn and rice harvests to the region's fish, poultry, and fruit sectors. Equipped with the standard DOST FIC machinery suite (spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, vacuum fryer), the center enables food producers to process, package, and commercialize value-added products to market standards.

Programs & Services

  • Food product development for agricultural raw materials: corn, rice, fish, fruits, and poultry products
  • Food processing using spray dryer, freeze dryer, water retort, and vacuum fryer
  • Food safety and regulatory compliance assistance (FDA registration, product labeling)
  • Packaging design and nutrition labeling support
  • Product costing and commercial feasibility analysis
  • Food product testing and quality evaluation
  • Equipment rental and processing facility access for MSMEs
  • MSME training in food technology, food safety, and value-chain development
