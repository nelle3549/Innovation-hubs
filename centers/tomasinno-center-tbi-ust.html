TOMASInno Center is the Technology Business Incubator (TBI) of the University of Santo Tomas (UST), located in Manila. Supported by DOST-PCIEERD, TOMASInno serves as UST's hub for technology entrepreneurship, startup incubation, and research commercialization — drawing on the vast academic resources of one of Asia's oldest universities (established 1611).

About the Center

The center's banner research area is Rehabilitation Engineering — a focus that reflects UST's strengths in medical and health sciences (College of Medicine, College of Nursing, Faculty of Medicine and Surgery). TOMASInno operates with a cross-faculty mentoring model, connecting startup teams with domain experts across UST's colleges of engineering, medicine, business, science, and allied health programs.

Programs & Services

  • Startup incubation for technology entrepreneurs, with priority in rehabilitation engineering and health technology
  • Cross-faculty mentoring from UST's colleges of Engineering, Medicine, Business, Science, and allied health programs
  • Business development coaching: business model development, market validation, and pitch preparation
  • IP guidance and technology transfer support for student inventors and startup founders
  • Physical incubation space and prototyping support within UST's Manila campus
