Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

FabLab DHVTSU

Pampanga State University (formerly Don Honorio Ventura Technological State University) · Region III

FabLab DHVTSU — now operating under Pampanga State University (PSU), formerly Don Honorio Ventura Technological State University — is the digital fabrication laboratory of Central Luzon's premier polytechnic state university in Bacolor, Pampanga. Managed under the university's Knowledge Management and Innovation Office (KMIO), the FabLab provides digital manufacturing tools and prototyping services to engineering students, faculty researchers, and MSMEs across Pampanga and the Central Luzon region.

About the Center

Listed on the global fablabs.io directory, the lab is aligned with the worldwide MIT-originated Fab Lab network's mission to democratize digital fabrication. As PSU's innovation infrastructure, it bridges classroom learning in engineering and technology programs with real-world product development — giving students hands-on prototyping experience while supporting local entrepreneurs and community innovators.

Located in Bacolor at the heart of Pampanga's industrial corridor, FabLab DHVTSU/PSU serves an MSME ecosystem rich in craft and manufacturing traditions, helping the region's entrepreneurs upgrade to modern digital production methods.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Additive manufacturing for product prototypes, engineering models, and custom components for students and MSMEs
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision laser processing of wood, acrylic, and other materials for product design, signage, and custom fabrication
  • CNC Milling/Routing — Computer-controlled machining for precision part fabrication, custom components, and mold making
  • Vinyl Cutting — Digital cutting for graphic applications, signage, labels, and product branding
  • 3D Scanning — Dimensional scanning for reverse engineering and digital modeling of physical objects
  • CAD Workstations — Digital design facilities for technical drawing, engineering design, and pre-production planning
  • Open-Access Fabrication — Services available to PSU students, faculty, and MSMEs across Pampanga and the Central Luzon region
