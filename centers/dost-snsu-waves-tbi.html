Technology Business Incubator

DOST-SNSU WAVES TBI

Surigao del Norte State University · Region XIII

DOST-SNSU WAVES TBI is a university-based Technology Business Incubator hosted by Surigao del Norte State University (SNSU) in Surigao City, Region XIII (Caraga). It was established in partnership with the DOST-Philippine Council for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology Research and Development (PCIEERD) and DOST Regional Office Caraga, and was officially recognized during the 6th TBI Summit in November 2022 as part of Philippine Startup Week.

About the Center

Funded under the Higher Education Institution Readiness for Innovation and Technopreneurship (HEIRIT) program, the WAVES TBI ranked highly among 27 higher education institutions that passed the technical panel and governing council evaluation. The center develops co-working spaces for startups, trains entrepreneurs with technology-related products and services, and helps build a vibrant innovation and technopreneurship ecosystem in the Caraga region.

Programs & Services

  • Business Incubation Program — Structured support for technology-based startups from ideation to market entry.
  • Pre-Incubation & Ideation — Early-stage guidance to help entrepreneurs validate and shape business concepts.
  • Co-Working Space — Dedicated workspace and facilities for incubatee startups.
  • Technopreneurship Training — Capacity-building on technology products, services, and business competencies.
  • Mentoring & Advisory — Coaching from mentors, industry partners, and technical experts.
  • Networking & Market Linkage — Assistance with networking, marketing, and connecting to industry and markets.
  • Fund Sourcing & DOST-PCIEERD Grant Linkage — Support in accessing DOST seed funding and startup grants.
  • IP & Technology Transfer Support — Guidance on intellectual property and commercializing innovations.
