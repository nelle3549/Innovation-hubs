Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

MSU-IIT FabLab Mindanao

MSU – Iligan Institute of Technology (MSU-IIT) · Region X

The MSU-IIT FabLab Mindanao is the digital fabrication laboratory of Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology (MSU-IIT), one of the premier state universities in Northern Mindanao. Established as part of the global Fab Lab network, the center serves students, researchers, MSMEs, and innovators in Iligan City and the surrounding region by providing open access to advanced digital fabrication and prototyping equipment.

About the Center

Located on the MSU-IIT campus in Iligan City, Lanao del Norte, FabLab Mindanao operates under the university's research and innovation mandate, supporting technology entrepreneurship, product development, and skills training in digital fabrication. The center's dedicated website (fablab.msuiit.edu.ph) and Facebook page (FABLABMindanao) reflect its active engagement with the maker and innovation community in Region X.

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of product concepts, custom parts, and scale models using FDM and similar technologies
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision cutting and engraving of acrylic, wood, cardboard, and other sheet materials
  • CNC Routing & Milling — Computer-controlled machining for wood, foam, and soft metals
  • Electronics Fabrication — Circuit board prototyping and embedded systems development support
  • Design Workstations — Computer-aided design (CAD) software access for product and engineering design
  • Prototyping Support — End-to-end guidance for MSMEs and student innovators developing physical product prototypes
  • Training & Workshops — Hands-on sessions on digital fabrication tools, maker skills, and design thinking for students and community members
