Technology Business Incubator

Bicol University TBI / SILI DELI

Bicol University · Region V

SILI DELI — Bicol University's Technology Business Incubator — is hosted by Bicol University in Legazpi City, Albay and is the first Technology Business Incubator established in the Bicol Region. Funded by DOST-PCIEERD under the HEIRIT Program and located at the BU Student Union Center, SILI DELI was purpose-built to support early-stage food innovation and technology-based business ventures in Bicol.

About the Center

The name SILI DELI is a creative homage to Bicolano culture: "sili" refers to Bicol's iconic chili pepper — a symbol of the region's identity — while "deli" is shorthand for delicatessen, reflecting the TBI's food-focused mission. Together, the name captures the center's character: distinctly Bicolano, creative, and centered on food as the primary vehicle for innovation and enterprise development.

SILI DELI's banner program is Food Engineering, reflecting Bicol University's engineering strengths and the Bicol Region's potential in food product development, food processing technology, and food value-chain innovation. The center provides high-growth food startups with co-working space, facilities, mentorship, and access to a dynamic ecosystem of innovation events — from hackathons and tech pitches to training programs and investor panels.

Programs & Services

  • Food Innovation & Technology Incubation — End-to-end incubation support for early-stage startups and MSMEs in food engineering, food processing technology, and Bicol-inspired food product development
  • Co-working Space & Shared Facilities — Physical office and co-working space at the BU Student Union Center, with access to shared production, testing, and development facilities for incubatee teams
  • Mentorship & Hands-on Coaching — Structured mentoring from food engineers, business development experts, and DOST advisors, providing guidance throughout the full startup development journey
  • Innovation Events & Ecosystem Building — Hackathons, technology pitching competitions, networking events, investor panels, and seminars to continuously surface new food-tech ventures and connect founders with stakeholders
  • Business Development Support — Assistance with business model design, financial planning, food product regulatory compliance, FDA registration, branding, and market entry strategy
  • DOST Funding & Investment Linkages — Access to DOST-PCIEERD startup grants, regional and national investor contacts, and industry partnerships for qualifying food-tech incubatee ventures
