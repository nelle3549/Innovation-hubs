Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

IDEA FabLab — Antipolo Institute of Technology

Antipolo Institute of Technology · Region IV-A

IDEA FabLab is the digital fabrication laboratory of the Antipolo Institute of Technology (AiTech), located in Antipolo City, Rizal in the CALABARZON region. The name IDEA reflects the center's focus on Innovation Design Engineering and Art — serving as AiTech's hands-on innovation hub for student engineers, technology entrepreneurs, and MSMEs in eastern Metro Manila and Rizal province.

About the Center

The center provides access to modern digital fabrication tools that bridge concept and prototype, enabling students and local entrepreneurs in Antipolo and Rizal to move from digital design to physical product without the costs typically associated with commercial prototyping services.

Programs & Services

  • 3D printing for product prototyping and design validation
  • Laser cutting and engraving for acrylic, wood, and material customization
  • CNC milling/routing for precision component fabrication
  • Vinyl cutting for signage, labels, and graphic applications
  • CAD workstations for digital design and product development
  • Open fabrication access for AiTech students and Antipolo/Rizal area MSMEs
  • Prototyping and rapid iteration support for technology startups and student innovators
