Fabrication Laboratory (FabLab)

USM-KCC FabLab

University of Southern Mindanao – Kidapawan City Campus · Region XII

The USM-KCC FabLab is the digital fabrication laboratory of the University of Southern Mindanao – Kidapawan City Campus (USM-KCC), located in Kidapawan City, Cotabato. As the first Fab Lab established in the SOCCSKSARGEN region (Region XII), USM-KCC FabLab plays a pioneering role in democratizing access to digital manufacturing and prototyping technology for students, researchers, MSMEs, and innovators in Central Mindanao.

About the Center

USM-KCC is part of the University of Southern Mindanao system, one of the major state universities in Mindanao's agricultural heartland. The FabLab aligns with the region's development priorities by providing entrepreneurs with the tools to prototype new agricultural products, packaging solutions, and value-added goods. The center is registered in the global fablabs.io directory and operates a dedicated Facebook page (facebook.com/usmkccfablab12).

Programs & Services

  • 3D Printing — Rapid prototyping of product concepts, agricultural tools, and custom mechanical parts
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving — Precision cutting and marking on acrylic, wood, and similar materials
  • CNC Milling & Routing — Computer-controlled machining for material shaping and component fabrication
  • Vinyl Cutting — Label, decal, and signage production for MSMEs and student projects
  • 3D Scanning — Digitization of physical objects for reverse engineering and digital archiving
  • Large-Format & UV Printing — Wide-format printing for display materials, packaging mockups, and exhibits
  • CAD Workstations — Design software stations for product development, engineering drawing, and 3D modeling
  • MSME Fabrication Support — Technical mentoring and equipment access for local businesses in the SOCCSKSARGEN area developing new products or packaging
