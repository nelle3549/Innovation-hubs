MSU-IIT Center of Innovation and Technopreneurship (CIT) — iDEYA

MSU – Iligan Institute of Technology (MSU-IIT) · Region X

The Center of Innovation and Technopreneurship (CIT), branded iDEYA, is the Technology Business Incubator of MSU – Iligan Institute of Technology (MSU-IIT), recognized as the first DOST-PCIEERD funded university business incubator in Mindanao. Located in Iligan City, Lanao del Norte, ILIGANiCE was established to transform MSU-IIT's engineering and technology research into commercially viable startups — linking the region's academic talent to markets, investors, and the national startup ecosystem.

About the Center

MSU-IIT is a nationally recognized Center of Excellence in engineering education and one of the country's strongest technical universities. ILIGANiCE builds on this foundation by providing structured incubation support to technology-based startups, student innovators, and technopreneurs from Northern Mindanao. The center has produced ventures in food technology, manufacturing, and other sectors, and its dedicated website (iliganice.msuiit.edu.ph) and Facebook page document its cohorts, milestones, and startup successes.

Programs & Services

  • Startup Incubation — Structured pre-incubation and incubation programs for technology-based startup teams from concept validation through prototype development and early market entry
  • Mentorship & Expert Coaching — Access to MSU-IIT faculty experts, industry practitioners, and DOST-PCIEERD mentors providing guidance on product development, business strategy, and commercialization
  • Engineering Facilities & Lab Access — Use of MSU-IIT's engineering laboratories, research equipment, and fabrication resources for startup product development and prototype validation
  • Business Development Support — Workshops and one-on-one coaching on business models, financial planning, market research, and investor pitching
  • IP & Legal Guidance — Support for intellectual property registration, technology licensing, and legal compliance for startup ventures
  • DOST-PCIEERD Program Linkage — Direct access to DOST Startup Grant Fund and other PCIEERD-funded commercialization programs for qualifying incubatees
  • Food Technology Innovation Track — Dedicated incubation support for food product startups, reflecting ILIGANiCE's track record with food technology ventures in Northern Mindanao
  • Alumni Startup Network — Active community of ILIGANiCE graduates providing peer mentoring, market connections, and collaborative opportunities for current incubatees
