Central Philippine University (CPU) · Region VI

CPU CPUGAD (CPU + PUGAD (Hiligaynon for “nest”)) Technology Business Incubator is hosted by Central Philippine University in Jaro, Iloilo City and serves Region VI (Western Visayas). Established through a ₱13.68 million grant from DOST-PCIEERD under the HEIRIT Program, CPUGAD TBI officially launched on 12 February 2025 as the only private university-based TBI in Region VI — bringing CPU's strengths in engineering and technology to the regional startup ecosystem.

About the Center

The name "CPUGAD" combines the university's initials with the Hiligaynon word "pugad" (meaning nest), reflecting the center's mission to nest and grow technology-based startups in Western Visayas. CPUGAD's banner program is integrating sustainable and creative ICT solutions for the food industry — making it a dedicated hub for food-tech startup development in the region.

CPUGAD TBI has rapidly earned national recognition: in 2025, it was named Outstanding TBI for Industry, Energy, and Emerging Technology at the Western Visayas Innovation Awards, and its incubatee startups RidA and NeoPark reached the Top 5 at PITCH 2025. The center has conducted international benchmarking in Thailand and is actively building regional innovation ecosystems through the TechNest Aklan collaboration with DOST-ASU.

Programs & Services

  • N³ Incubation Program — CPUGAD's flagship multi-stage incubation track designed to take technology startups from concept through prototype to market-ready ventures with structured mentorship and milestones
  • Central Launch — A formal startup launch platform providing incubatees with visibility, investor connections, and media exposure as they transition from incubation to commercial operation
  • IdeaHatch — An ideation and early-stage validation program for aspiring entrepreneurs with promising concepts but still in the pre-startup phase
  • Crack IT & Hackathons — Competitive innovation events and problem-solving challenges that surface new startup ideas and recruit promising entrepreneurs into the incubation pipeline
  • Fin-X — Financial readiness and fundraising preparation program connecting startups with funding agencies, grant programs, and investor networks
  • Nest Circle & BIRD's i-VIEW — Mentorship circles and investor-ready demonstration programs providing incubatees with expert coaching, peer support, and pitch rehearsal with funding partners
  • DOST Funding & Regional Ecosystem Linkages — Access to DOST-PCIEERD grants, connections to the Western Visayas innovation ecosystem, and regional benchmarking partnerships for scaling startup ventures
